What To Notice in This Simulation:
  • Quadratic Technical Losses: Lowering the Power Factor from $0.98$ to $0.75$ dramatically increases technical losses because current $I = P / (\sqrt{3} V \cos \phi)$, dissipating heat via $I^2 R$.
  • The AT&C Multiplier: Even with a 90% billing efficiency, an 85% collection efficiency yields an AT&C loss of $1 - (0.90 imes 0.85) = 23.5\%$, threatening utility solvency.
  • AI Triage Precision: By correlating line resistance with consumer billing variances, the unsupervised anomaly detector isolates real meter tampering while avoiding false accusations on long, lossy feeders.
