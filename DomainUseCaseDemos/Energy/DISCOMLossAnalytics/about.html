1. The Core Problem: Why DISCOMs Face Financial Insolvency

Power Distribution Companies (DISCOMs) globally face severe revenue leakage due to Aggregate Technical & Commercial (AT&C) losses. In emerging markets, AT&C losses often exceed 20–30%, draining billions in working capital and stalling renewable energy integration.

Traditional utility accounting struggles to answer a fundamental operational question: "When energy vanishes between the 11kV substation and household meters, how much was lost as physical heat in conductors versus stolen through meter tampering and unpaid bills?"

2. Mathematical & Engineering Formulations

2.1 The Official AT&C Loss Formulation (Central Electricity Authority / CEA)

AT&C loss combines operational metering accuracy with financial collection performance:

Billing Efficiency (ηb) = Total Energy Billed (kWh) / Total Energy Input (kWh)

Collection Efficiency (ηc) = Amount Realized (INR/$) / Total Amount Billed (INR/$)

AT&C Loss Rate (%) = [ 1 - (ηb × ηc) ] × 100

2.2 Physical Technical Losses (Ohmic Copper & Core Dissipation)

Technical line loss across a 3-phase medium voltage (11kV) feeder follows Ohm's and Joule's laws:

Current Irms = Pactive / ( √3 × Vline × cos φ )

Ploss, technical = 3 × Irms2 × Rfeeder × t

Notice that as the Power Factor ($\cos \phi$) deteriorates, current $I$ surges non-linearly to deliver the same active power, causing $I^2R$ technical losses to explode quadratically.

2.3 Commercial & Non-Technical Losses (Theft & Tampering)

Energytheft = Energyinput - Energytechnical_loss - Energybilled

3. How AI & Smart Meter Telemetry Solve the Problem

Before Advanced Metering Infrastructure (AMI), utilities conducted random field raids, achieving less than a 10% hit rate and antagonizing consumers. Modern AI architectures use:

  • Energy Balance Auditing: Comparing feeder-head smart meters with downstream aggregate Distribution Transformer (DTR) meters to isolate the exact lossy branch.
  • Unsupervised Anomaly Detection (Isolation Forests & Autoencoders): Detecting sudden drops in billing ratio that cannot be explained by ambient temperature or industrial seasonality.
  • Phase Discrepancy & Shunt Detection: Flagging suspicious zero-current nights alongside active transformer loads.

4. Classroom Exercises & Discussion Prompts

  1. The Power Factor Experiment: In the interactive demo, set Technical Resistance to $1.5 ext{x}$. Then move the Power Factor slider from $0.98$ down to $0.72$. Observe how AT&C loss rises without adding any consumer theft. What capital expenditure (e.g. capacitor banks) can DISCOMs deploy to fix this?
  2. The False Positive Trade-Off: Increase the AI Anomaly Sensitivity to $0.95$. How many DTRs are flagged? If a field inspection crew costs ₹5,000 per visit, calculate the net ROI of auditing the top 5 vs top 20 flagged transformers.
  3. Policy Implications: Why does a utility with 95% billing efficiency and 90% collection efficiency still suffer a 14.5% AT&C loss? How does this composite metric protect bondholders?
