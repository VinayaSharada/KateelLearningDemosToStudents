Teacher observation guide

What to look for, observe, and note

Use these prompts to turn the demo result into a guided learning conversation.

What to look for

Look for realistic customer segments, account types, transaction patterns, and liquidity metrics that mirror real banking data.

What to observe

Observe how transaction velocity varies by segment, how liquidity ratios differ across account types, and how risk scores are distributed.

What to note

Note that synthetic data quality improves with appropriate seed values and realistic parameter ranges that match institutional patterns.

Learning design

What students should learn

This page is the teaching guide; the linked demo is the hands-on practice environment.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand how synthetic financial data models real-world banking patterns.
  • Identify customer segments, account types, and transaction behaviors that affect liquidity.
  • Compare baseline metrics with AI-assisted insights for liquidity risk assessment.
  • Explain one limitation of synthetic data for real treasury decision-making.

Concepts covered

●Customer segmentation
●Account balances & liquidity
●Monte Carlo forecasting
●Risk scoring & VaR

Classroom flow

Suggested 30-minute activity

Before the demo

  1. Ask students what patterns they expect in banking transaction data.
  2. Predict how different customer segments affect liquidity ratios.
  3. Clarify that generated data is for simulation, not real transactions.

During the demo

  1. Launch the demo and generate data for 1000 customers and 10000 transactions.
  2. Toggle AI insights to explain liquidity risk patterns in the generated data.
  3. Run Monte Carlo forecast and compare scenarios.

After the demo

  • Export the generated CSV and share one insight.
  • Explain why modeling assumptions matter for treasury planning.
  • Rate this About Demo page to help improve the catalog.

Faculty guide

Prompt for discussion or assessment

Ask students to generate data, run a Monte Carlo forecast, then identify which customer segment poses the highest liquidity risk and propose a mitigation strategy.
