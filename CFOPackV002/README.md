# CFO Decision Lab

The CFO Decision Lab is now delivered as a private, facilitated cohort
experience.

The flagship program combines:

- the **CFO Liquidity War Room**, where teams build and defend a 30-day
  liquidity decision; and
- the **Transformation Investment Committee**, where teams transfer the same
  evidence discipline into a finance-transformation investment decision.

Visit the [program page](index.html) for outcomes, format, a sample decision
canvas, and registration contact.

The earlier public notebooks and workshop engine have been retired from the
current public site. Previously published community-edition releases remain
historical material and are not the maintained premium program.
