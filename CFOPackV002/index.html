This is not a Run All demonstration. Your team selects a confidence view, collections strategy, supplier extension, inventory release, facility draw and per-currency hedge ratios. Those choices alter the downstream forecast and the approval request.

What you produce

A concise approval request linked to model evidence, action cost, funding and FX downside.

30-day control plan

Named owners, completion evidence, monitoring targets and escalation paths.

Before using company data

The case is fully synthetic. Company use requires controlled mapping of receivables, payments, outflows, supplier constraints, inventory options, facility terms, FX exposures and approval policy. Read the custom-data guide before replacing any input.
