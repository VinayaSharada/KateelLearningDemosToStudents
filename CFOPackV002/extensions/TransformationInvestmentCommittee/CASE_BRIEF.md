# Case Brief — Northstar Components

Northstar Components is a fictional USD 1.4 billion industrial group operating
through six legal entities and three ERP environments. The Board has authorized
up to USD 8 million for the first wave of finance transformation, but will
release the second wave only after a credible 90-day proof point.

## Current state

| Signal | Current position |
|---|---|
| Group close | 11 calendar days |
| Supplier invoices manually touched | 68% |
| Forecast refresh | 12 business days after month-end |
| Chart-of-account mappings | 17 local-to-group mappings |
| Management-report reconciliation | 420 hours per month |
| Analytics and automation roles | Two vacancies; no product owner |

## Board mandate

- Commit no more than USD 8 million in Wave 1.
- Fund no more than two initiatives concurrently.
- Produce one visible, controlled proof point within 90 days.
- Do not automate a materially defective process.
- Do not deploy decision-generating AI without source traceability and a named
  human approver.
- Preserve the ability to stop, revise or scale at the Day-90 gate.

## Your decision

Choose one initiative for `NOW`, one for `NEXT`, and place the remaining options
in `LATER` or `DO NOT FUND`. Different answers may be defensible. Your Board
memo must quantify value, acknowledge confidence, explain dependencies, and
state what evidence will unlock the next investment.
