# CFOPackV002 Pre-Work

Complete this 30-45 minute preparation before the facilitated workshop.

## 1. Know the decision

The CFO is not asking for a model demonstration. The CFO needs a 30-day
liquidity recommendation with explicit actions, cost, downside, authority and
monitoring triggers.

## 2. Assign team roles

- **Treasurer:** owns the cash forecast and funding recommendation.
- **Controller:** challenges data, assumptions and traceability.
- **Commercial challenger:** protects customer and supplier relationships.
- **CFO spokesperson:** integrates and defends the final decision.

Teams of two or three may combine roles. Every participant must challenge at
least one assumption during the final defence.

## 3. Review these concepts

- Contractual date versus expected cash date
- Minimum liquidity and committed-facility headroom
- Mean absolute error and a simple model benchmark
- DSO, supplier-term and inventory-release cash effects
- Receivable versus payable FX direction
- Hedge ratio, downside reduction and hedge cost

## 4. Bring

- A laptop with Colab access or local Python/Jupyter
- The participant workbook
- A calculator or spreadsheet for independent checks
- One real example of a forecast assumption that proved wrong

No API key is required for the core workshop.

## 5. Pre-work reflection

Write one sentence for each:

1. What minimum liquidity would your organization protect?
2. Which cash assumption is least reliable in your current process?
3. When would you prefer committed funding over operational action?
4. What evidence should be required before changing an FX hedge?
