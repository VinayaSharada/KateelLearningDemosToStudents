# After the Liquidity War Room

## Within 24 hours

- Save the final decision charter and decision paper.
- Record every unresolved assumption and its owner.
- Confirm that no synthetic result is represented as company advice.

## Within one week

- Map one real data extract using `CUSTOM_DATA_GUIDE.md`.
- Rebuild the contractual forecast independently.
- Identify which receipt-timing baseline can be backtested.
- Confirm minimum liquidity, facility and hedge-policy inputs.

## Within 30 days

- Compare forecast to actual receipts and outflows.
- Measure action effectiveness and direct cost.
- Record policy exceptions and approval latency.
- Conduct a lessons-learned review before reusing the model.

The reusable capability is not a fixed answer. It is the discipline of tracing a
liquidity decision from evidence through action, authority and monitoring.
