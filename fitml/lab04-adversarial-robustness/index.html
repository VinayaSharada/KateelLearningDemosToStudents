Self-Service Lab Workflow

Step 1: Open Google Colab

Launch the verified notebook directly in Google Colab. PyTorch, torchvision, and Grad-CAM libraries load automatically.

Step 2: Generate Grad-CAM

Hook into the final convolutional feature maps (e.g. ResNet-50 layer4) to produce class activation maps for sample images.

Step 3: Craft FGSM Attacks

Compute input gradients with PyTorch backward pass and craft single-step FGSM perturbations bounded by ε.

Step 4: Adversarial Training

Implement the Madry PGD min-max inner loop to harden model weights and plot robust vs clean accuracy tradeoffs.
