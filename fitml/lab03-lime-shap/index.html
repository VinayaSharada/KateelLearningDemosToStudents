Self-Service Lab Workflow

Step 1: Open Google Colab

Launch the verified notebook directly in Google Colab. Dependencies (shap, lime, xgboost) are pre-configured.

Step 2: Train Gradient Boosted Model

Fit an XGBoost / LightGBM model on the tabular German Credit and Diabetes benchmark datasets.

Step 3: Compute TreeSHAP

Generate exact TreeSHAP waterfall and beeswarm summary plots. Compare top global factors against individual predictions.

Step 4: LIME Local Surrogates

Generate local perturbation samples, fit sparse Ridge regressors, and compare explanation stability across perturbations.
