Self-Service Lab Workflow

Step 1: Open Google Colab

Launch the verified notebook directly in Google Colab. The notebook auto-installs scikit-learn, pandas, numpy, and fairness metric modules.

Step 2: Inspect Datasets

Load the COMPAS and Adult Income datasets from the fitml/data/ folder. Inspect demographic feature distributions and label base rates.

Step 3: Evaluate Baseline

Train a baseline classifier and calculate Disparate Impact and Equalized Odds. Observe the statistical disparity between groups.

Step 4: Pre-Processing Mitigation

Apply sample reweighting and evaluate the new model. Document the trade-off in accuracy versus fairness metrics for your assignment.
