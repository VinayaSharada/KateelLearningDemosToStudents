Learning Objectives

What students learn in this lab

1. Constrained In-Processing

Formulate multi-objective optimization problems where prediction loss is minimized subject to equality or inequality fairness constraints (Lagrangian duality).

2. Adversarial Debiasing

Set up an adversarial min-max game where a primary network predicts the outcome while an adversary attempts to infer protected attributes from internal representations.

3. Causal DAGs & Confounding

Draw and inspect structural causal models (Pearl's do-calculus) to distinguish permissible business mediators from impermissible proxy discrimination pathways.

4. Counterfactual Fairness

Mathematically audit whether an individual's decision outcome would remain invariant in a counterfactual world where their sensitive attribute had been different.

Theoretical Foundations

In-Processing & Causal Formulations

Constrained Loss Formulation

Minimize cross-entropy loss $\mathcal{L}_{task}$ subject to fairness penalty parameter $\lambda$:

min_θ L_task(y, ŷ) + λ · |P(ŷ=1|A=0) - P(ŷ=1|A=1)|

Tuning $\lambda$ controls the frontier between pure predictive accuracy and group demographic parity.

Counterfactual Fairness Condition

A predictor $Ŷ$ is counterfactually fair if for all individuals $X=x$ and attributes $A=a$:

P(Ŷ_{A ← a} = y | X = x, A = a) = P(Ŷ_{A ← a'} = y | X = x, A = a)

Changing $A$ to $a'$ in the causal graph must not change the distribution of the prediction.

Adversarial Min-Max Objective

The prediction head minimizes prediction loss while confusing the discriminator:

min_W max_U [ L_pred(y, f_W(x)) - γ · L_adv(a, g_U(f_W(x))) ]

Removes linear and non-linear mutual information regarding $A$.

Faculty Teaching Prompts

Classroom Discussion & Reflection

During Class Activity

Guide students through the causal DAG editor. Have them remove the direct arrow from Age to Credit Approval while preserving the mediated arrow through Credit History, and observe the resulting model behavior.

Ethical & Legal Prompt

"If an attribute is causally correlated with risk due to historical societal injustice, is a model that uses that attribute 'fair' because it is causally accurate, or 'unfair' because it perpetuates historical harm?"
