Self-Service Lab Workflow

Step 1: Open Google Colab

Launch the verified notebook directly in Google Colab. Dependencies and German Credit datasets are automatically loaded.

Step 2: Causal Modeling

Specify the structural equations for the credit risk process and evaluate conditional independence (d-separation) on the data.

Step 3: Adversarial Training

Train a neural network with a gradient reversal layer (GRL) and evaluate whether the discriminator can infer sensitive demographic status.

Step 4: Counterfactual Audit

Synthesize counterfactual pairs ($X_{A \leftarrow 0}$ vs $X_{A \leftarrow 1}$) and verify whether decision outputs flip unjustly.
