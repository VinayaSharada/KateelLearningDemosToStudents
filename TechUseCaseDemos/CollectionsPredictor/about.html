Learning design

What students should learn

This page is the teaching guide; the linked demo is the hands-on practice environment.

Learning outcomes

  • Understand the core treasury management concept behind Collections Predictor.
  • Identify the decision, data input, and output that matter in the activity.
  • Compare a baseline result with an AI-assisted or scenario-adjusted result.
  • Explain one limitation or governance consideration before using the output.

Concepts covered

●Liquidity forecasting
●Scenario planning
●Working-capital trade-offs
●Treasury governance

Classroom flow

Suggested 20-minute activity

Before the demo

  1. Ask students what decision the demo is supporting.
  2. Predict what will happen when one input changes.
  3. Clarify that AI output is support, not final authority.

During the demo

  1. Launch the actual demo and change at least two inputs or scenarios.
  2. Toggle AI support on/off where available and compare what changed.
  3. Record one insight, one limitation, and one follow-up question.

After the demo

  • Share one screenshot or exported result.
  • Explain the decision logic in plain language.
  • Rate this About Demo page to help improve the catalog.

Faculty guide

Prompt for discussion or assessment

Ask students to run a stress scenario, export or screenshot results, then defend the treasury action in a 90-second board memo.
