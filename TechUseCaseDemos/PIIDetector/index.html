What this demo is about

Compliance teams must audit documents for exposed PII before sharing with regulators or partners. This demo shows what an automated detector finds—and what it misses.

Learning objectives

  • Understand what constitutes PII and why it matters.
  • See detection limits: false positives, missed entities.
  • Learn why automated detection requires human review.

How this works

  • Text input: Paste or type any text to analyze.
  • PDF upload: Upload a single PDF file. Text is extracted automatically.
  • Detection: A transformer-based NER model identifies potential PII entities.
  • Confidence: Each entity has a confidence score. Higher = more likely to be real PII.
  • Review: Always review detected PII with human judgment. Models make mistakes.
