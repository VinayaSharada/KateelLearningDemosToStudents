What you can enhance on your own

  • Add more claims, contradictory evidence rows, and reviewer-signoff states.
  • Add exportable release decisions with owner assignments and unsupported-claim notes.
  • Add role views for preparer, reviewer, approver, and audit follow-up.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample commentary and evidence with the reporting questions relevant to your context.
  • Decide what counts as sufficient source traceability before finance release.
  • Keep the demo focused on evidence sufficiency rather than assuming generated commentary is trustworthy by default.

Audience fit

This demo supports CFO, controllership, audit, faculty, and student audiences because the same evidence question appears in many reporting and governance settings.
