# Finance Evidence and Data-Lineage Challenge

Browser-based finance evidence demo for tracing commentary back to source rows and deciding whether the output is fit for CFO release.

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more claims, conflicting evidence rows, and reviewer-signoff states.
- Add exportable release decisions with unsupported-claim notes and owner assignments.
- Add role views for preparer, reviewer, approver, and audit follow-up.
- Add a second scenario showing partial evidence, stale evidence, or contradictory source data.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the sample commentary and row-level evidence with the reporting, disclosure, or management-pack questions relevant to your context.
- Decide what counts as sufficient source traceability before a finance output can be released.
- Keep the demo focused on evidence sufficiency rather than treating generated commentary as automatically trustworthy.
- Use the same demo across CFO, controllership, audit, faculty, and student settings by changing the reporting case around the shared evidence logic.

## Business decision

Use this demo to make the central decision in Finance Evidence and Data-Lineage Challenge explicit, surface the key trade-offs, and decide how you would adapt the workflow, assumptions, or outputs in your own context.
