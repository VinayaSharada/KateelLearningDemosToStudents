# Dell vs Competitors Working Capital Case

## Overview

Browser-based treasury case comparing Dell, HP, Lenovo, and Apple using revenue, DSO, DIO, DPO, and cash conversion cycle.

## Learning objectives

- Explain how receivables, inventory, and payables shape cash conversion cycle.
- Estimate how much cash is tied up or released by operating-policy changes.
- Compare which working-capital lever is easiest versus hardest to copy competitively.
- Discuss where aggressive payables strategy creates financial benefit but operational risk.

## How to run

- Browser: open `index.html`.

## Who this is for

- CFOs and aspiring CFOs
- Treasury, FP&A, and controllership teams
- Faculty using a case-style working-capital discussion
- Students comparing operating-policy trade-offs

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more peers, scenarios, and company-specific presets beyond the current base, Dell-discipline, and supply-shock views.
- Add side-by-side sensitivity analysis for DSO, DIO, and DPO so the cash-release trade-off is even clearer.
- Add exports for board-style summary notes, management actions, and downside-risk commentary.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the sample company assumptions with your own business-unit, peer-set, or industry data.
- Revalidate revenue, DSO, DIO, and DPO definitions before using the outputs in an internal discussion.
- Use the case as a decision-support view, not as a substitute for commercial, supply-chain, or supplier-relationship judgement.
