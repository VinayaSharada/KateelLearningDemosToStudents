Business decision

Decide whether the CFO should fund the initiative now, phase it, or hold until benefit evidence and control readiness improve.

What this demo adds

The calculator now includes implementation cost, process capacity released, adoption curve, control-remediation cost, benefit-confidence range, and payback period.

Judgement boundary

The model supports an investment discussion, but final capital allocation and finance approval remain human decisions.

Facilitator mode

Use the built-in 5-8 minute mode to show how adoption and confidence shift payback and recommendation quality.

Participant exercise

Use the 10-minute mode to ask learners to change assumptions and decide whether the initiative should be approved, phased, or held.

Expected answer

A strong answer covers confidence-adjusted value, payback, control readiness, ownership, and the approval condition required before funding.

Self-service reuse

Strengthen the assumption set before you strengthen the approval case

What you can enhance on your own

  • Add scenario bands, hurdle-rate options, and multi-year adoption paths.
  • Add richer benefit categories and exportable decision packs.
  • Add side-by-side conservative, base, and aggressive funding cases.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample assumptions with your own investment and benefit drivers.
  • Separate measurable benefits from strategic upside before governance use.
  • Keep the calculator advisory until finance and business owners agree on the value logic.

Audience fit

This demo supports CFO, finance transformation, AI/ML, product, and classroom audiences because the funding decision logic is widely reusable.

Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional finance-transformation assumptions to illustrate business-case logic, not to prescribe a real funding decision.

Human approval point

Finance and business owners still need to validate benefits, remediation cost, adoption realism, and payback before investment approval.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the structure, scenario framing, and value logic, but rework the thresholds, owners, and evidence requirements for your own governance model.
