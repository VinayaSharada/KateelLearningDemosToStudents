# Instructor Guide

## Business decision

Decide whether a finance-transformation initiative is strong enough to fund now, stage in phases, or hold until controls, adoption, or benefit confidence improve.

## Facilitator mode

Time: `5-8 minutes`

1. Start with the default finance-transformation scenario.
2. Show how implementation cost, capacity release, adoption curve, and control-remediation cost affect value.
3. Ask whether the payback period is acceptable for the CFO.
4. Highlight that confidence range and governance evidence matter before approval.

## Participant exercise

Time: `10 minutes`

Ask participants to:

1. Change one or two assumptions.
2. Compare the base case with the conservative case.
3. Decide whether to approve now, phase the rollout, or hold.
4. State the owner, approval point, and what evidence is still needed.

## Teaching cues

- Capacity release is only valuable if the business can actually redeploy it.
- AI benefit claims should be treated as a range, not a single point estimate.
- Controls and remediation costs belong in the business case.
- Human approval remains necessary even when the economics look strong.
