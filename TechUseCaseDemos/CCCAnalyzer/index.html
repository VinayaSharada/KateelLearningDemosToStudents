📖 Learning Guide: Cash Conversion Cycle

DSO (Days Sales Outstanding): Average days to collect receivables. Lower = better cash flow.
Inventory Days: Average days inventory sits. Lower = less carrying cost.
DPO (Days Payable Outstanding): Average days to pay suppliers. Higher = more free financing.
CCC = DSO + Inventory Days - DPO: Lower CCC = better working capital efficiency.
