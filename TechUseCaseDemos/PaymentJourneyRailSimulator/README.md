# Payment Journey and Rail Simulator

Browser-based anchor demo for the Digital Payments course pack.

## What it teaches
- Participants and message flow by rail
- Authorization, clearing, and settlement differences
- Time to finality and reversal logic
- Fee and liquidity implications
- Fraud exposure and experience trade-offs

## Run mode
Open `index.html` in a browser or via GitHub Pages.

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more rails or corridor variants such as correspondent banking, stablecoin, or CBDC-linked flows.
- Add explicit side-by-side comparisons for customer experience, merchant economics, and operational recovery burden.
- Add exportable rail-comparison summaries for class discussion or internal workshops.
- Add a mode that separates domestic versus cross-border compliance and liquidity steps more visibly.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Choose the rails that matter in your market and replace the sample fee or timing assumptions with local scheme realities.
- Revalidate what "finality" means in your context: customer-visible success, scheme settlement, bank funding, or legal recoverability.
- Use the simulator to support treasury, payments, product, risk, or faculty discussion without changing the core rail logic.
- Keep course-specific sequencing outside the demo so the same simulator remains reusable across multiple learning paths.
