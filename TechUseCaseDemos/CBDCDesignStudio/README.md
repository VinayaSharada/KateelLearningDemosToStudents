# CBDC Design Studio

Browser-based Digital Payments demo for:

- retail vs wholesale CBDC
- token vs account
- direct, hybrid, or intermediated architecture
- privacy and AML trade-offs
- offline support
- limits, interest, programmability, and cross-border options

## Run mode

Open `index.html` in a browser or via GitHub Pages.

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add jurisdiction-specific policy presets for retail, wholesale, and cross-border CBDC designs.
- Add explicit stakeholder scorecards for central bank, commercial bank, merchant, citizen, and regulator perspectives.
- Add side-by-side scenario export so learners can compare two CBDC designs rather than only one at a time.
- Add more visible trade-off logic around privacy, offline resilience, bank-disintermediation risk, and programmability limits.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the sample design assumptions with the policy questions, institutional constraints, and legal priorities relevant to your country or course.
- Decide which design dimensions are descriptive and which represent contested policy judgments before using it for executive discussion.
- Use the studio across payments, public-policy, banking, treasury, or classroom settings by changing the debate prompt rather than the core design controls.
- Keep the underlying simulator course-neutral and put institutional recommendations in the surrounding teaching material.

## Business decision

Use this demo to make the central decision in CBDC Design Studio explicit, surface the key trade-offs, and decide how you would adapt the workflow, assumptions, or outputs in your own context.
