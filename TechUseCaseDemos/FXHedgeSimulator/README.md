# FX Hedge Strategy Optimizer

## Overview
Browser-based hands-on exercise for FX Risk & Hedging (Session 2).

## Learning Objectives
- Compare hedge instrument trade-offs
- Understand risk-return balance
- Experience AI-driven decision support
- Practice hedging strategy optimization

## Features
- **FX Exposure Calculator**: Spot rate, exposure amount, time horizon inputs
- **Hedge Instrument Selector**: Forwards, Options, Swaps, Natural Hedge
- **Strategy Comparison Matrix**: 50%, 75%, 90% hedge comparison
- **AI Insights**: Market condition-based recommendations
- **Export Functionality**: Download results as JSON

## How to Run
1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Select a market scenario from dropdown
3. Adjust exposure parameters
4. Choose hedge instruments
5. Review strategy comparison and AI insights
6. Export results if needed

## Scenarios Included
| Scenario | Market Condition |
|----------|------------------|
| Stable Market | Low volatility, steady rates |
| High Volatility | Large price swings |
| Strong Trend | Directional market movement |
| Market Crisis | Extreme uncertainty |
| Custom | User-defined parameters |
