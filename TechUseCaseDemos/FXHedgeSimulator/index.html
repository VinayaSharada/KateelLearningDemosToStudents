💱 FX Hedge Simulator

Interactive FX hedging strategy simulator with scenario analysis for currency exposure management and risk mitigation.

🌐 Browser-Based | 💰 Treasury Management | ⚙️ Intermediate

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand FX exposure types and measurement
  • Learn hedging instruments and strategies
  • Practice scenario analysis for currency risk
  • Master cost-benefit analysis of hedging

🚀 Key Features

  • Exposure Calculator: Transaction, translation, and economic exposure
  • Hedging Tools: Forward contracts, options, currency swaps
  • Scenario Engine: Market volatility simulations
  • Cost Analyzer: Hedging cost vs. risk reduction trade-off

📋 Learning Modules

  • Module 1: FX Exposure Identification
  • Module 2: Hedging Strategy Selection
  • Module 3: Cost-Benefit Analysis
  • Module 4: Scenario Planning
