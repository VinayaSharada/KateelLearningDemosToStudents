About: Project Anvil — Due-Diligence Data Room

Concept, learning objectives and how to adapt this demo to your own case

Overview

A fictional acquirer evaluates a fictional precision-machining target at an ₹85 cr ask — and the seller's numbers genuinely support it (₹86.4 cr at 6× reported EBITDA). Then diligence takes the story apart: eight red flags, a valuation bridge down to ₹62.8 cr, and a bear case of ₹24 cr. Nobody lied; the annual numbers were true. That is the lesson.

The architecture idea (the part worth stealing)

Institutionalize the argument. In the live tier, a Deal Sponsor agent is required to make the strongest honest bull case and an Adversarial Auditor is required to attack it — while a deterministic scanner supplies the findings and neither agent may invent a number. The IC-chair agent adjudicates on evidence and recommends; the committee of humans decides.

Learning objectives

  • Grain reveals what aggregates hide: annual revenue was flat and true; the monthly volume-vs-price split exposed a shrinking anchor account behind price increases.
  • Findings must land as price: a risk without a ₹ impact is an opinion — the bridge converts eight findings into one negotiating position.
  • Clause risk lives in documents: read the change-of-control extract the legal agent read; it makes 44% of revenue conditional on this very deal.

The business decision it supports

Proceed at ₹85 cr, renegotiate at ≤₹59.6 cr with four conditions precedent, or walk?

Run modes

Browser (this page): pre-computed findings, bridge, scenarios and contract extracts; no setup, no keys.
Live tier (registered participants): the full data-room scanner, Streamlit app, and the sponsor-vs-auditor agent committee (Anthropic / OpenAI / OpenRouter / Groq via a .env file), re-runnable on a fresh seed per cohort — or on an anonymized real deal.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

The live tier validates nine CSV contracts (financials, monthly revenue by customer, customers, related parties, contracts, HR census, asset register, litigation, receivables aging) plus markdown contract extracts. Load your last real deal, anonymized, and see what the scan finds; deal terms (ask, multiple) live in one YAML file.
