Business decision

Decide whether a finance AI use case is ready for controlled deployment, should remain pilot-only, or requires remediation before approval.

What this demo adds

The scorecard now reflects CFO control needs instead of a generic AI checklist.

Judgement boundary

The tool identifies gaps, but deployment approval and accounting judgement remain human decisions.

Facilitator mode

Use the built-in 5-8 minute mode to highlight the weakest controls and discuss whether deployment should proceed.

Participant exercise

Use the 10-minute mode to vary control ratings, decide on deployment readiness, and name the required remediation path.

Expected answer

A strong answer identifies the weakest control, owner, evidence gap, and the approval condition that must be met.

Self-service reuse

Clarify the control model before you broaden deployment

What you can enhance on your own

  • Add more control domains, policy presets, and remediation exports.
  • Add stakeholder-specific views for CFO, audit, and technology owners.
  • Add clearer deployment-state comparisons.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample control ratings with your own governance criteria.
  • Decide which gaps are acceptable for pilot use versus production release.
  • Keep the scorecard advisory until control owners confirm evidence and approval rights.

Audience fit

This demo supports CFO, audit, compliance, AI/ML, and classroom audiences because control readiness is a reusable governance question.

Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional control ratings and sample evidence patterns to illustrate governance review, not to certify a real deployment.

Human approval point

Control owners, finance leaders, and approvers must still confirm traceability, judgement boundaries, and approval rights before release.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the scorecard structure and remediation discussion, but replace the thresholds, owners, and evidence standards with your own policy model.
