# Participant Task

## Scenario

You are helping the CFO review an AI-enabled finance process before broader rollout.

## Your task

1. Review the scorecard.
2. Adjust control ratings where needed.
3. Decide whether the process is ready for controlled deployment, pilot-only use, or remediation first.
4. State the main control gap and the approval condition.

## Deliverable

Provide a short answer covering:

- deployment decision
- top control weakness
- owner
- audit evidence needed
- approval point
