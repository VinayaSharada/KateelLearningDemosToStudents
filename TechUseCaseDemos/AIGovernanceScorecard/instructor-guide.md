# Instructor Guide

## Business decision

Decide whether a finance AI use case is ready for controlled deployment, needs remediation before approval, or must remain in pilot mode.

## Facilitator mode

Time: `5-8 minutes`

1. Walk through the default control ratings.
2. Show which finance controls are weak.
3. Ask whether the CFO should approve deployment.
4. Reveal the expected answer and discuss remediation priorities.

## Participant exercise

Time: `10 minutes`

Ask participants to:

1. Change the control ratings.
2. Identify the weakest governance area.
3. Decide whether deployment should proceed.
4. State the owner, approval path, and audit evidence needed.

## Teaching cues

- A high technology score is not enough without finance control design.
- Accounting judgement boundaries must stay with finance and controllership.
- Audit evidence and exception ownership matter as much as model quality.
- Human-in-the-loop is a real control only when approval rights are explicit.
