# Ind AS Contract Review Workbench

Browser-based contract review demo for extracting finance questions while keeping final accounting judgement with finance and legal.

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more contract clauses such as rebates, renewal rights, variable consideration, or financing components.
- Add side-by-side clause extraction versus accounting-judgement views so users can see the boundary more clearly.
- Add reviewer notes, owner fields, and exportable issue lists for legal and finance follow-up.
- Add a second scenario with conflicting clauses or missing commercial context.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the sample contract questions with the Ind AS, revenue, lease, or policy issues relevant to your organization or course.
- Decide which extracted signals are useful prompts and which require deeper legal or accounting review before action.
- Keep the workbench focused on surfacing finance questions rather than implying automated accounting judgement.
- Use the same demo across finance, legal, audit, faculty, and classroom settings by changing the contract case around the shared judgement-boundary logic.

## Business decision

Use this demo to make the central decision in Ind AS Contract Review Workbench explicit, surface the key trade-offs, and decide how you would adapt the workflow, assumptions, or outputs in your own context.
