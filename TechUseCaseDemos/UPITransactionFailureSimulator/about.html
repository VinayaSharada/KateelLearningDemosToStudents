What this demo is about

UPI is fast, but fast does not mean simple

This demo focuses on the operational state machine around UPI: what happens when one participant thinks the payment is done and another still sees it as pending, duplicated, or under review.

Learning objectives

  • Compare push and collect flow responsibilities.
  • Explain why pending and timeout states create support and reconciliation costs.
  • Connect fraud controls and limits to customer-friction trade-offs.

Best fit

Use this after the rail simulator, once students already know the major UPI participants and now need to see where operational exceptions appear.

Teacher cue

Ask whether the right KPI is raw speed, low fraud, low complaints, or low unresolved pendings. Good payment operations usually require trading off all four.
