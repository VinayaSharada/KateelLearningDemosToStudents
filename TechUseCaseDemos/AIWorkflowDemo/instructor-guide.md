# Instructor Guide

## Business decision

Decide how a finance AI workflow should route exceptions, where escalation belongs, and which step requires human approval.

## Facilitator mode

Time: `5-8 minutes`

1. Show the default workflow.
2. Compare the AI step with the human approval step.
3. Ask whether the design is safe enough for finance.
4. Discuss the exception route and approval gate.

## Participant exercise

Time: `10 minutes`

Ask participants to:

1. Switch between scenarios.
2. Identify the highest-risk handoff.
3. Decide what must remain manual.
4. State the owner and escalation point.
