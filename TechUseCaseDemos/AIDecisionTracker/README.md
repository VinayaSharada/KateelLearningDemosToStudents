# AI Decision Tracker

Browser-based finance decision-record demo for deciding whether an AI-enabled initiative has enough ownership, evidence, risk, and approval detail to move forward.

## Decision focus

The demo is designed around one finance question:

`Is the decision record complete enough for finance leadership to review and approve this initiative responsibly?`

## Included guidance files

- `about.html`
- `index.html`
- `instructor-guide.md`
- `participant-task.md`
- `expected-answer.md`

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more governance fields such as dependency, funding source, model owner, or post-approval monitoring.
- Add exportable decision-record packs for steering committee or audit review.
- Add status transitions for draft, pilot, conditional approval, and approved with monitoring.
- Add stakeholder views for CFO, CIO, process owner, and faculty discussion.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the sample roles and approval states with the governance structure used in your organization or course.
- Decide what evidence is required before a decision record becomes approval-ready.
- Keep the tracker as a record-quality tool until human approvers confirm the decision and escalation path.
- Use the same demo across finance governance, AI/ML, transformation, and classroom settings by changing the initiative case around the shared decision-record structure.
