Business decision

Decide whether a finance AI initiative is documented well enough for review and approval.

What this demo adds

The tracker now uses a reusable finance decision record template instead of a generic note log.

Judgement boundary

The tool organizes the record, but final approval remains with human decision-makers.

Self-service reuse

Broaden the governance record before you broaden the approval claim

What you can enhance on your own

  • Add more governance fields, status transitions, and exportable review packs.
  • Add stakeholder views for CFO, CIO, process owner, and faculty discussion.
  • Add monitoring and post-approval follow-up fields.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample roles and approval states with your own governance structure.
  • Decide what evidence is required before a record becomes approval-ready.
  • Keep the tracker as a record-quality tool until human approvers confirm the decision.

Audience fit

This demo supports finance governance, AI/ML, transformation, and classroom audiences because the decision-record question is broadly reusable.
