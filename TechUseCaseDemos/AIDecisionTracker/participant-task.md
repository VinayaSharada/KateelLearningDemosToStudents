# Participant Task

## Scenario

You are supporting a finance-transformation steering review for an AI-enabled initiative.

## Your task

1. Review the decision record.
2. Add or update ownership, evidence, and risk fields.
3. Decide whether the initiative is ready for approval.
4. State what must be reviewed next and by when.

## Deliverable

Provide a short answer covering:

- approval readiness
- key risk
- initiative owner
- approval path
- review date
