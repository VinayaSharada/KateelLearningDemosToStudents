# Instructor Guide

## Business decision

Decide whether a finance AI initiative has a decision record strong enough to move forward, or whether the record lacks ownership, evidence, risk, or approval detail.

## Facilitator mode

Time: `5-8 minutes`

1. Review the default finance decision record.
2. Highlight missing ownership, evidence, or approval items.
3. Ask whether the decision is ready to proceed.
4. Reveal the expected answer and discuss accountability.

## Participant exercise

Time: `10 minutes`

Ask participants to:

1. Add or edit a finance decision record.
2. Decide whether the record is approval-ready.
3. Identify the biggest governance gap.
4. State the owner and review date.

## Teaching cues

- A decision log is useful only when ownership is explicit.
- Evidence, risk, and approval fields are what make the log auditable.
- CFO sponsor, CIO owner, process owner, and data owner should not be collapsed into one role.
