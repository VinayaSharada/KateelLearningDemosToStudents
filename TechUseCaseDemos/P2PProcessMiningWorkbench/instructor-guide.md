# Instructor Guide

## 8-minute run of show

1. Show the assumed AP happy path and ask where invoices probably wait.
2. Reveal the actual variants and ask what the designed process failed to show.
3. Filter missing-PO and missing-GRN loops and ask whether the problem is automation or upstream discipline.
4. Show wait time, exception ageing, and high-value cases.
5. Surface the control violation where payment-ready status was reached before evidence completion.
6. Use the decision board to classify actions as eliminate, standardize, enable, assure, or monitor.
7. Close on the monitoring triggers and owner accountability.

## Key teaching message

Process mining is a diagnostic layer. It reveals how work behaved, but management still has to decide what must be redesigned upstream, what can be automated, and what must remain controlled and human-reviewed.
