    Glossary

    • Case: one procure-to-pay instance.
    • Variant: one unique ordered activity sequence across cases.
    • Wait time: elapsed time between events, less illustrative touch-time assumptions.
    • Conformance: whether the executed path respected the intended control and process logic.

    Fictional disclaimer

    All data, customers, entities, targets, SLA assumptions, and cost figures are fictional and illustrative. Event-log analysis reveals behaviour patterns, not audited root cause.
