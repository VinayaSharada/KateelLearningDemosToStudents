What you can enhance on your own

  • Add budget scenarios, stakeholder-specific scoring, and alternative Year 1 / Years 2-3 portfolio paths.
  • Add exportable first-wave, deferred-wave, and stop decisions.
  • Add clearer board packs for CFO, CIO, process-owner, and data-owner viewpoints.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample initiatives and scoring assumptions with your own transformation choices.
  • Decide which dimension matters most in your context: value, readiness, resistance, control risk, dependency, or budget.
  • Keep the simulator advisory until the organization validates dependencies, sponsorship, execution capacity, and proof-point expectations.

Audience fit

Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional initiative choices, scoring assumptions, dependency rules, and capacity limits to illustrate sequencing logic rather than approve a real portfolio.

Human approval point

Leadership still needs to validate dependencies, sponsorship, delivery risk, 90-day proof points, and budget trade-offs before committing to a transformation wave.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the sequencing structure and viewpoint framing, but replace the initiative definitions, budgets, and governance thresholds for your context.
