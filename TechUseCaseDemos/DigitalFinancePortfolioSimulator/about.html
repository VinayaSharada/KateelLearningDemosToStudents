How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample initiatives and scoring assumptions with your own transformation choices.
  • Decide which dimension matters most in your context: value, readiness, resistance, control risk, or budget.
  • Keep the simulator advisory until the organization validates dependencies, sponsorship, and execution capacity.

Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional initiative choices, scoring assumptions, and capacity limits to illustrate sequencing logic rather than approve a real portfolio.

Human approval point

Leadership still needs to validate dependencies, sponsorship, delivery risk, and budget trade-offs before committing to a transformation wave.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the scenario comparison and prioritization structure, but replace the weights, initiative definitions, and governance thresholds for your context.
