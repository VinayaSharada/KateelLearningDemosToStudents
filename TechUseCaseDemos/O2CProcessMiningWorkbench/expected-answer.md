# Expected Answer

## Strong answer pattern

The strongest answer usually identifies credit-hold rework, delivery-dispute loops, or short-payment review as the biggest source of avoidable delay, then separates process redesign from technology enablement.

## Example answer

- Eliminate or standardize: tighten credit-evidence policy and master-data discipline so orders do not enter repeated credit review without accountable ownership.
- Enable: once the stable path is clear, use workflow for approval routing, reminders, and escalation on standard invoices or unapplied-cash follow-up.
- Keep human-reviewed: short-payment review and control-violation cases still need controller or collections judgement because evidence and deduction context matter.
- Monitor: track straight-through-processing rate, queue time by owner, unresolved high-value cases, and any case where cash was applied before evidence was complete.

## What to avoid

- Treating every delay as a bot opportunity.
- Ignoring upstream discipline because the exception volume looks small.
- Assuming mined behaviour proves root cause without policy and owner review.
