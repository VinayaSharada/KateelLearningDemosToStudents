# Lite Parse Demo

Browser-based finance parsing demo using a fictional contract and invoice pack to extract payment terms, rebates, return rights, and renewal clauses while keeping accounting judgement explicitly human.

## Business decision

Use this demo to decide which contract or invoice clauses deserve finance, legal, or controllership review before someone treats extracted text as if it were already an accounting conclusion.

## Who this is for

- CFOs and aspiring CFOs
- Controllers, legal teams, and revenue-review owners
- Faculty and course designers
- Students learning the extraction-versus-judgement boundary

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more clause types such as volume rebates, financing components, termination rights, or variable consideration triggers.
- Add reviewer ownership, approval status, and exception-routing fields to turn the output into a reusable review log.
- Add side-by-side views that separate extracted signals from accounting judgement, legal judgement, and commercial follow-up.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the fictional contract and invoice pack with sanitized source text from your own policies, contracts, or invoice annexures.
- Align the extracted clause labels to the checklist your finance or legal team already uses.
- Keep the warning visible: extraction can surface issues, but revenue recognition and contract interpretation remain human decisions.
