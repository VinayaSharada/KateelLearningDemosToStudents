What this demo is about

Extraction can speed review, but judgement still belongs to people

This demo helps learners separate three different things that are often blurred together: text extraction, issue spotting, and final finance or legal judgement. The output should trigger better review, not replace it.

Business decision

Decide which clauses require finance, legal, or controllership review before anyone treats the extracted text as an accounting conclusion.

Learning objectives

  • Identify payment terms, rebates, return rights, and renewal clauses from a fictional source pack.
  • Explain why extracted text is not the same as accounting or legal judgement.
  • Discuss which clauses create follow-up for revenue, collections, or contract review teams.

Audience fit

This demo works for CFO, controllership, legal, faculty, and student audiences because the core question is reusable across many courses and operating settings.

Self-service reuse

Improve the review workflow before you imply stronger automation

What you can enhance on your own

  • Add more clause types such as financing components, rebates, termination rights, or variable consideration triggers.
  • Add ownership, reviewer notes, and exportable issue logs so the demo becomes a reusable review record.
  • Add side-by-side views that separate extraction, accounting judgement, and legal judgement more clearly.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the fictional pack with sanitized clauses from your own contract or invoice-review context.
  • Map the extracted labels to the checklist already used by your finance or legal reviewers.
  • Keep the warning explicit: extraction is a screening aid, not a substitute for accounting judgement.
