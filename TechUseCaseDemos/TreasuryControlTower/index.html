🏢 Treasury Control Tower

Browser-based liquidity stress testing simulator with AI-powered insights for treasury modernization and cash flow forecasting.

🌐 Browser-Based | 💰 Treasury Management

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand liquidity forecasting importance
  • Experience AI value in risk detection
  • Learn scenario planning methodology
  • Practice cash flow projection skills

🚀 Key Features

💰 Cash Flow Panel - Editable inflows/outflows with seasonal adjustment
🌪️ Stress Testing - 5 predefined scenarios plus custom options
📊 Liquidity Dashboard - Real-time cash position visualization
🤖 AI Comparison - Shows risk with and without AI insights

📋 Scenarios Included

ScenarioDescriptionImpact
Normal OperationsStandard business conditionsBaseline
Revenue Decline (-15%)Economic downturnCash shortage risk
Payment AccelerationEarly supplier paymentsIncreased cash outflow
Emergency OutflowUnexpected investmentLiquidity stress
CustomUser-defined parametersYour own analysis

🎓 How to Use

  1. Open index.html in a browser
  2. Select a stress scenario from dropdown
  3. Adjust cash flow inputs as needed
  4. Toggle AI insights on/off
  5. View results and export if needed
