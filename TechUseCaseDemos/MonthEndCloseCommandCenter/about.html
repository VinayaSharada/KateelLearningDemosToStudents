What you can enhance on your own

  • Add close-calendar milestones, evidence logs, and more exception classes.
  • Add role views for controller, CFO, shared services, and audit.
  • Add exportable close-decision records and facilitator prompts.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample exceptions and materiality thresholds with your own close policy.
  • Decide which breaks truly block close and which can remain open under monitored remediation.
  • Keep the output advisory until finance owners confirm the approval and no-autonomous-posting rules.

Audience fit

This demo can support controllership, CFO, internal-audit, shared-services, and classroom audiences because the core decision is reusable: can close proceed, and what evidence is still missing?

Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional close exceptions, evidence states, and thresholds to illustrate close governance rather than a live close decision.

Human approval point

Controllers and finance owners must still decide whether unresolved items are material, whether close can proceed, and whether any posting restriction remains necessary.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the exception framing, owner fields, and approval logic, but replace the thresholds, evidence rules, and close policy with your own standards.
