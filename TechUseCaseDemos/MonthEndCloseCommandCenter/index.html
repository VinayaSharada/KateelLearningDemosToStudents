Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional entity statuses, exception values, and evidence states to illustrate close governance rather than a live close decision.

Human approval point

Controllers and finance owners still decide whether close proceeds, whether a posting restriction remains, and whether risk acceptance is appropriate.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the exception structure and agent-trace concept, but replace thresholds, owners, and approval logic with your own close policy.
