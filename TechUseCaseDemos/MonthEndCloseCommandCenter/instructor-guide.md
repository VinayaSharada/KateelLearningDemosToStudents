# Instructor Guide

Use this demo to show that AI can summarize close status, but controller approval and posting authority remain human.
Use facilitator mode to move quickly through four questions:

1. Which exceptions are genuinely material?
2. Which items are evidence problems versus timing problems?
3. What can the agent draft or escalate safely?
4. What still requires controller approval before close proceeds?

Encourage participants to compare a cautious controller response with a more commercial “close and remediate later” stance, then discuss why the no-autonomous-posting rule remains non-negotiable.
