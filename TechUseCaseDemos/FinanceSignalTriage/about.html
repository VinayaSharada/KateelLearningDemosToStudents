How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample signal types and corroboration metrics with the indicators relevant to your context.
  • Decide which combinations truly require escalation and which should remain monitored or closed with evidence.
  • Keep the demo focused on corroborated judgement rather than treating soft signals as self-sufficient evidence.

Audience fit

This demo supports CFO, treasury, FP&A, risk, faculty, and student audiences because the signal-versus-evidence question is widely reusable.

Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional signals, corroborating metrics, review states, and escalation routes to teach triage logic rather than trigger real escalations.

Human approval point

Teams still need to judge whether corroboration is sufficient, who owns the issue, and whether escalation, monitoring, investigation, or closure is appropriate.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the signal-versus-evidence framing, but align the thresholds, owners, and escalation rules with your own risk and finance processes.
