What you can enhance on your own

  • Add more evidence rows, unsupported-claim types, and reviewer states.
  • Add exportable review records and owner assignments.
  • Add side-by-side scenarios for stronger evidence versus faster production.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample finance narrative and evidence base with the reporting questions relevant to your context.
  • Decide what level of citation and review is required before release.
  • Keep the summarizer focused on reviewer support rather than implying automatic readiness.

Audience fit

This demo supports CFO review, controllership, audit, AI/ML, and classroom audiences because the evidence-versus-release question is broadly reusable.
