# Reusable Finance Skills Studio

Browser-based demo for comparing ad hoc prompting with a governed reusable finance skill.

## Business decision

Use this demo to decide whether a finance skill is governed well enough for controlled internal use or whether it needs stronger evidence rules, refusal rules, or reviewer design.

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more refusal rules, output templates, and source types.
- Add skill version history and more acceptance tests.
- Add role-specific reviewer views for FP&A, controllership, and CFO release.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the fictional management-pack scenario with your own sanitized reporting case.
- Keep reviewer approval mandatory.
- Treat the skill as governed drafting support, not autonomous finance judgement.
