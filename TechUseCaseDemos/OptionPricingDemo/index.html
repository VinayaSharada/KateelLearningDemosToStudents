💰 Option Pricing Demo

Black-Scholes options pricing calculator with Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho). Master derivatives pricing and risk management.

🌐 Browser-Based | 💰 Quant Finance | 📈 Intermediate

🎯 Learning Objectives

  • Understand Black-Scholes model assumptions
  • Calculate option pricing and Greeks
  • Learn risk management strategies
  • Practice volatility impact analysis

🚀 Key Features

  • Black-Scholes Calculator: Call and put option pricing
  • Greeks Display: Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega, Rho
  • Volatility Smile: Implied volatility surface
  • Monte Carlo Simulation: Path-dependent options

📋 Input Parameters

  • Spot Price (S)
  • Strike Price (K)
  • Time to Maturity (T)
  • Risk-free Rate (r)
  • Volatility (σ)
  • Dividend Yield (q)
