Risk Parity Formula

Weight_i = (σ_i)^(-1) / Σ_j (σ_j)^(-1)

Each asset's risk contribution = Weight_i × σ_i / Σ_j (Weight_j × σ_j)

Key Insight: Lower volatility assets get higher weights!
