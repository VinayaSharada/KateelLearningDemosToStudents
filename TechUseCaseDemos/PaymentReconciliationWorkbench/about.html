Payment Operations and Reconciliation Workbench

A CFO-facing exception-management demo that asks whether finance can close, who owns the break, what evidence is needed, and where escalation must happen.

Launch Demo View course path View README

Business decision

Decide whether the controller can allow close or whether unresolved exceptions should block release until evidence and approval are complete.

What this demo adds

The workbench now highlights unmatched value, ageing of exceptions, materiality threshold, root cause, owner, SLA, escalation outcome, and explicit human approval.

Judgement boundary

The demo can recommend attention areas, but it does not approve close. Finance retains the final close decision.

Facilitator mode

Use the built-in 5-8 minute mode to demonstrate the highest-risk exception, show the evidence path, and discuss whether close should proceed.

Participant exercise

Use the 10-minute mode to ask learners to vary the scenario, identify material exceptions, assign ownership, and choose an escalation outcome.

Expected answer

A strong answer identifies unmatched value, materiality, root cause, owner, SLA, escalation path, and the approval condition needed before close.
