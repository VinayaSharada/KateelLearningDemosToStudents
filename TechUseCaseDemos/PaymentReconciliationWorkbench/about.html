Payment Operations and Reconciliation Workbench

A CFO-facing exception-management demo that asks whether finance can close, who owns the break, what evidence is needed, and where escalation must happen.

Launch Demo View course path View README

Business decision

Decide whether the controller can allow close or whether unresolved exceptions should block release until evidence and approval are complete.

What this demo adds

The workbench now highlights unmatched value, ageing of exceptions, materiality threshold, root cause, owner, SLA, escalation outcome, and explicit human approval.

Judgement boundary

The demo can recommend attention areas, but it does not approve close. Finance retains the final close decision.

Facilitator mode

Use the built-in 5-8 minute mode to demonstrate the highest-risk exception, show the evidence path, and discuss whether close should proceed.

Participant exercise

Use the 10-minute mode to ask learners to vary the scenario, identify material exceptions, assign ownership, and choose an escalation outcome.

Expected answer

A strong answer identifies unmatched value, materiality, root cause, owner, SLA, escalation path, and the approval condition needed before close.

Self-service reuse

Extend the queue only after the control logic is clear

What you can enhance on your own

  • Add more exception classes such as FX differences, cut-off errors, and tax mismatches.
  • Add file-level comparison views before the summarized queue is created.
  • Add exportable reconciliation packs with owner, SLA, and approval notes.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace the sample thresholds and owner roles with your finance close standards.
  • Decide which exceptions truly block close in your environment.
  • Keep the output advisory until finance owners confirm the evidence path and approval rights.

Audience fit

This workbench supports CFO, controller, treasury, payments-operations, audit, and faculty audiences because the core question is reusable: what evidence is sufficient to let the organization proceed?

Control boundary

What remains illustrative, and what must stay human

Data and evidence note

This demo uses fictional payment, gateway, network, and bank mismatches to teach reconciliation decisions without exposing live transaction files.

Human approval point

Automation can route or classify stable mismatches, but controller approval remains necessary before close proceeds on material items.

Safe reuse guidance

Reuse the exception classes and handoff logic, but replace thresholds, owners, and close policy with your own payment-control model.
