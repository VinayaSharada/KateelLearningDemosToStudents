# Expected Answer

## Strong answer pattern

- Close should be blocked when pending reversals or settlement gaps create unresolved money movement or liability uncertainty above the materiality threshold.
- Fee-only mismatches may be monitored if they are below threshold and clearly owned.
- The owner should be explicit, for example payment operations, gateway operations, or finance controls.
- The controller approval point should remain human and should require evidence that the exception is resolved, immaterial, or provisioned correctly.

## What a good participant answer includes

- unmatched value
- materiality judgement
- root cause
- owner
- SLA
- escalation outcome
- approval condition
