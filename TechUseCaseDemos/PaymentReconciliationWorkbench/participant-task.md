# Participant Task

## Scenario

You are supporting the CFO and controller at period end. Payment files from the merchant, gateway, network, and bank do not fully agree.

## Your task

1. Generate the exception queue.
2. Review the CFO summary.
3. Decide:
   - Is the unmatched value material?
   - Which root cause matters most?
   - Who owns the next action?
   - Can close proceed, or should it be blocked?

## Deliverable

Give a short answer covering:

- close decision
- top exception class
- owner
- SLA
- escalation outcome
- evidence needed before approval
