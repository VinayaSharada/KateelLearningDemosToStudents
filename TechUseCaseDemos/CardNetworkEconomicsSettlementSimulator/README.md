# Card Network Economics and Settlement Simulator

Browser-based Digital Payments demo for:

- cardholder → merchant → acquirer → network → issuer economics
- interchange and MDR
- gateway and acquirer costs
- settlement delay
- refunds, chargebacks, and fraud loss
- merchant-margin impact

## Run mode

Open `index.html` in a browser or via GitHub Pages.

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add issuer, network, acquirer, and merchant waterfall exports for scenario comparison.
- Add cross-border card use, FX spread, or installment logic if those are relevant to your audience.
- Add side-by-side scenarios for low-MDR/high-chargeback versus high-MDR/low-fraud economics.
- Add a merchant-segment view so students can compare thin-margin and high-margin businesses.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the sample MDR, interchange, gateway, and fraud assumptions with local network or merchant realities.
- Validate whether settlement delay, chargeback timing, and fraud-loss ownership match your jurisdiction and product type.
- Use the demo across payments, merchant-acquiring, treasury, or strategy teaching by changing the business question, not the participant chain.
- Keep the core simulator course-neutral and move institution-specific policy commentary into facilitation notes.

## Business decision

Use this demo to make the central decision in Card Network Economics and Settlement Simulator explicit, surface the key trade-offs, and decide how you would adapt the workflow, assumptions, or outputs in your own context.
