What this demo is about

Card payments are a fee-and-risk chain, not just an approval signal

This demo turns card economics into a visible participant waterfall so students can see where the merchant discount goes and how later disputes distort apparent revenue.

Self-service reuse

Extend the waterfall, then localize the economics

What you can enhance on your own

  • Add richer waterfall exports for merchant, issuer, network, and acquirer economics.
  • Add cross-border, FX, installment, or merchant-segment variants.
  • Add side-by-side scenarios that compare margin and fraud trade-offs more clearly.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

  • Replace sample MDR, interchange, gateway, and fraud assumptions with your local card economics.
  • Revalidate chargeback timing, settlement delay, and loss ownership for your market.
  • Keep the participant chain stable and move institution-specific policy discussion into facilitation notes.

Audience fit

This demo is reusable across payments, merchant-acquiring, treasury, strategy, and classroom settings because it makes the economics visible to every participant rather than only one side of the scheme.
