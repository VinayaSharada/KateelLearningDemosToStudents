# AI Cost-Benefit Analyzer

## Learning Objectives
- Calculate total cost of AI development projects
- Estimate business value from AI automation
- Understand ROI and payback period for AI investments
- Make data-driven decisions about AI project viability

## How to Run
1. Open `index.html` in a browser
2. Enter development costs (data, compute, personnel)
3. Enter expected business volume and efficiency gains
4. Click "Calculate ROI" to see financial analysis

## Financial Metrics
- **Total Development Cost**: Sum of data, compute, and personnel costs
- **Annual Business Value**: Monthly volume × value per transaction × efficiency gain × 12
- **Net Present Value (3 years)**: Discounted cash flow analysis
- **ROI**: Return on investment percentage
- **Payback Period**: Months to recover initial investment
