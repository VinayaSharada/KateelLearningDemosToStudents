Smart Contract Treasury

Students can route stablecoin treasury transfers through an approval workflow, compare operating scenarios, and observe how automated controls reduce manual risk.

Runs fully in browser Scenario-based wallet balances Approval simulation

What this demo is about

Treasury teams using digital assets need policy-driven approvals, visibility into balances, and guardrails for large transfers.

Learning objectives

  • Understand balance-aware transaction execution.
  • See why high-value transfers need stricter approval controls.
  • Compare manual review and AI-assisted monitoring.

How to use the demo

Step 1

Choose an operating scenario and enable or disable AI security guidance.

Step 2

Enter a recipient, amount, and token, then submit a transaction.

Step 3

Approve or reject the queued payment and explain the governance rationale.

What you can enhance on your own

Add richer policy thresholds, approval states, and exportable decision records before turning the workflow into a more automated treasury-control lab.

How to adapt this demo to your use case

Map the wallet, token, and approval rules to your own treasury policy, and keep human approval in place wherever the governance boundary is still contested.

Who this can serve

This demo is useful to treasury, digital-assets, controls, risk, and classroom audiences because it exposes approval logic rather than hiding it behind automation jargon.
