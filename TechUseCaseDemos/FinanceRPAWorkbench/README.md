# Finance RPA Workbench

Browser-based finance RPA demo for comparing manual AP rekeying with a controlled bot path.

## Business decision

Use this demo to decide whether AP work should be standardized first, automated for a stable path, or kept human-owned because policy or exception design is not mature enough.

## What you can enhance on your own

- Add more invoice classes, ERP touchpoints, and retry rules.
- Add side-by-side workflow versus RPA comparisons.
- Add richer evidence logs and exception-aging scenarios.

## How to adapt this demo to your use case

- Replace the fictional invoices and exception logic with your own sanitized AP scenarios.
- Keep approval, payment release, and policy override explicitly human-owned.
- Validate materiality, reviewer assignment, and fallback rules before reuse.
