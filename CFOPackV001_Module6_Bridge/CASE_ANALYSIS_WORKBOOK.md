# PART 1: WORKED EXAMPLE - Asteron Digital Finance Priority Case

## Case Overview

**Company:** Asteron Industrial India (INR 4,800 crore manufacturer)  
**CFO:** Ananya Rao  
**Decision:** Which digital finance initiative to fund as 90-day proof point?  
**Budget:** INR 28 crore  
**Timeline:** Must show credible value in 90 days

---

## Step 1: Import & Validate (Current State)

### What We Know from the Case

| Element | Current State | Evidence from Case |
|---------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Finance Structure** | 7 legal entities, 4 ERP instances | Multiple systems, inconsistent data |
| **Close Timeline** | 14 calendar days | Pain point: slow reporting |
| **Manual Work** | 41% of journal entries | Pain point: controls, FTE cost |
| **Forecasting** | Refresh 18 days after month-end | Pain point: slow decision-making |
| **Data Quality** | 12 chart-of-account mappings | Pain point: no consistent definitions |
| **Automation** | Email approvals | Pain point: audit trail, compliance |
| **Talent** | No defined data/analytics roles | Pain point: capability gap |
| **Budget** | INR 28 crore | Constraint: limited resources |

### Maturity Assessment (From Case Exhibit 1)

| Dimension | Score/5 | Implication |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Data governance | 2.0 | Foundation is weak; data issues will cascade |
| Process automation | 1.5 | Lots of manual work; high risk, high opportunity |
| Analytics | 2.5 | Analytics exists but lags; slow time-to-insight |
| Talent fluency | 2.0 | Team needs reskilling; adoption risk is real |

**Validation Summary:** Asteron has classic finance transformation pain—multiple systems, slow close, manual processes, weak data foundation. This is a typical target for digital finance investment.

---

## Step 2: Baseline Forecast (Current Pain)

### What Does "Doing Nothing" Cost?

Calculate the annual impact of current pain points:

| Pain Point | Current State | Annual Cost/Impact |
|------------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Long close** | 14 days vs. industry best practice (5 days) | 9 days × FTE effort × 12 months = ~120 FTE days/year (~INR 2.4 cr salary cost) |
| **Manual journals** | 41% manual vs. best practice (10%) | ~31% excess FTE + error rework + audit queries = ~INR 1.8 cr/year |
| **Slow forecasting** | 18 days late vs. best practice (same day) | Decisions delayed; missed opportunity cost = ~INR 0.8 cr/year |
| **Poor data quality** | 12 COA mappings = reconciliation + rework | ~INR 0.6 cr/year in reconciliation FTE |
| **Compliance risk** | Email approvals; no audit trail | Potential audit findings; remediation cost = ~INR 0.3 cr/year |
| **Talent frustration** | High turnover; reskilling cost | Turnover cost + external hiring premium = ~INR 1.0 cr/year |
| **TOTAL ANNUAL PAIN** | — | **~INR 7.0 crore/year** |

**Key Insight:** Asteron is spending INR 7 crore/year in inefficiency costs from finance dysfunction. Any initiative that removes even 30% of this pain pays for itself.

---

## Step 3: Collections Intelligence (Analyze Each Initiative)

### What Impact Does Each Option Promise?

From case Exhibit 2, extract the promised benefits:

| Initiative | Cost (INR cr) | Primary Benefit | Pain Point It Solves | Estimated Impact | Confidence |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|---------------------|------------------|-----------|
| **Finance Data Foundation** | 22 | Consistent reporting definitions | Weak data quality (12 COA mappings) | Unlocks 3+ initiatives; foundation benefit | Medium (benefits diffuse) |
| **AP Workflow Automation** | 12 | Faster invoice cycle + audit trail | 41% manual journals + compliance | ~15% reduction in manual effort | High (clear, measurable) |
| **Close Redesign** | 9 | Shorter close + fewer manual journals | 14-day close + 41% manual | ~5 days faster + 20% fewer manuals | High (immediate visible) |
| **Predictive Planning Pilot** | 18 | Faster scenario analysis | 18-day forecast lag | Decisions 18 days earlier; better accuracy | Medium (data quality risk) |

**Analysis:**
- **Data Foundation** solves the ROOT problem but benefits aren't immediately visible (risk: "we spent money, where's the value?")
- **AP Automation** is the quickest win (INR 12 cr, high confidence, 90-day ROI visible)
- **Close Redesign** is the most visible (14 → 9 days is obvious to CFO and Board)
- **Predictive Planning** depends on data quality first (high risk if data is weak)

## Step 4: Revised Forecast (Model Each Scenario)

### 90-Day Outcome for Each Initiative

**Scenario A: Fund Data Foundation (INR 22 cr)**

| Timeline | Outcome |
|----------|---------|
| Day 1-30 | Consolidate 12 COA mappings into 4; define data governance; pilot in one entity |
| Day 31-60 | Migrate 2nd and 3rd entities; establish master data; build self-service analytics |
| Day 61-90 | Full group reporting; design follow-on initiatives (automation, planning) |
| **Day 90 Proof Point** | ✅ Group reporting is consistent; ❌ but AP cycle time unchanged; ❌ close still 14 days |
| **Visible to Board?** | **Maybe** — finance team sees value, but CFO might not have external result to show |
| **Adoption Risk** | **High** — "Why are we cleaning data if it doesn't speed up close or reduce manual work?" |

**Scenario B: Fund AP Automation (INR 12 cr)**

| Timeline | Outcome |
|----------|---------|
| Day 1-30 | Select tool (e.g., SAP Intelligent Automation); define AP workflow; pilot on 20% of invoices |
| Day 31-60 | Expand to 60% of invoices; measure cycle time reduction; audit trail live |
| Day 61-90 | Full rollout; achieve 80% 3-way match automation; measure error reduction |
| **Day 90 Proof Point** | ✅ Invoice cycle: 8 days → 4 days; ✅ Audit trail 100%; ✅ 20% FTE savings (~INR 0.5 cr/year) |
| **Visible to Board?** | **YES** — AP team processes invoices 50% faster; tangible cost savings |
| **Adoption Risk** | **Low** — Users see immediate benefit (faster processing, fewer errors) |

**Scenario C: Fund Close Redesign (INR 9 cr)**

| Timeline | Outcome |
|----------|---------|
| Day 1-30 | Map current close process; identify manual steps; redesign workflow; pilot with 1 entity |
| Day 31-60 | Automate inter-company reconciliations; simplify journal entry templates; roll to 3 entities |
| Day 61-90 | Full group close on new process; measure timeline; calculate FTE savings |
| **Day 90 Proof Point** | ✅ Close timeline: 14 days → 8 days; ✅ 30% fewer manual journals; ✅ 15% FTE savings (~INR 0.3 cr/year) |
| **Visible to Board?** | **YES** — Monthly close finishes 6 days earlier; dramatic for reporting cycles |
| **Adoption Risk** | **Medium** — Cross-entity process change; some resistance from controllers |

**Scenario D: Fund Predictive Planning Pilot (INR 18 cr)**

| Timeline | Outcome |
|----------|---------|
| Day 1-30 | Build planning data foundation; source data from 4 ERP instances; create data pipes |
| Day 31-60 | Train ML model on historical actuals; test scenario generation; validate accuracy |
| Day 61-90 | Live scenario planning; run 10x more "what-if" analysis than before |
| **Day 90 Proof Point** | ✅ Forecasts 18 days faster; ✅ BUT data quality issues cause prediction errors; ❌ CFO questions accuracy |
| **Visible to Board?** | **Maybe** — Faster planning is good, but forecast accuracy is medium (data quality drag) |
| **Adoption Risk** | **HIGH** — If predictions are off, users lose trust; "garbage in, garbage out" |

## Step 5: Working Capital Levers (Trade-Off Analysis)

### Comparison Matrix: Which Initiative Wins on Cost/Benefit?

| Factor | Data Foundation | AP Automation | Close Redesign | Planning Pilot | Winner |
|--------|-----------------|---------------|-----------------|----------------|--------|
| **Cost (INR cr)** | 22 | 12 | 9 | 18 | **Close Redesign** |
| **Day 90 Value Visible?** | Medium | High | High | Medium | **AP/Close** |
| **ROI Horizon** | 18+ months | 6 months | 9 months | 12+ months | **AP Automation** |
| **Adoption Ease** | Hard | Easy | Medium | Medium | **AP Automation** |
| **Enables Next Initiative?** | Yes (enables all) | No | No | No | **Data Foundation** |
| **Compliance Risk Reduction** | Low | High | Medium | Low | **AP Automation** |
| **Control Risk** | Medium | Low | Low | High | **AP/Close** |

### The Trade-Off Story

**The Dilemma:** "Which initiative creates a credible proof point without weakening control?"

- **Data Foundation** is the strategic foundation (solves root cause) but slow to show value
- **AP Automation** is the quick win (obvious ROI, low risk, highest adoption) but doesn't solve close timeline
- **Close Redesign** is the visible win (6-day improvement is dramatic) but doesn't solve manual FTE cost broadly
- **Predictive Planning** is ambitious but risky (data quality will undermine accuracy)

---

## Step 6: FX Hedging Decision (Risk & Control)

### Risk Assessment for Each Initiative

| Initiative | Control Risks | Mitigation | Adoption Risk | Mitigation |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Data Foundation** | Governance gaps during migration; data loss risk | Phased rollout; backup strategy; audit; 3rd-party governance consultant | High — "Why are we spending on data cleanup?" | Position as "enabler for all future initiatives" |
| **AP Automation** | Automation of inconsistent practices; invoice exceptions | Strong controls BEFORE automation; exception management process; SAP audit trail | Low — users love faster processing | Quick training; early wins = adoption |
| **Close Redesign** | Inter-company journal errors during redesign; control gaps in manual reviews | Process documentation; control mapping; UAT with controllers; Audit pre-approval | Medium — controllers resist change | Show time savings = capacity for analytics |
| **Predictive Planning** | Forecast accuracy depends on data quality; model risk | Independent audit of source data; forecast vs. actual tracking; conservative assumptions | High — if forecasts are wrong, trust erodes | Require data foundation FIRST |

**Key Control Decision:** AP Automation and Close Redesign strengthen controls (audit trail, fewer manual steps). Data Foundation requires investment before payoff. Planning Pilot has execution risk.

---

## Step 7: Decision Framework (The Recommendation)

### Ananya's Recommendation

Based on the 8-step analysis, here's what Ananya should recommend to the Board:

---

#### **RECOMMENDATION: Fund AP Workflow Automation as the 90-Day Proof Point**

**Rationale in 3 Points:**

1. **Highest confidence of success** — AP automation is the most predictable initiative. Industry benchmarks show 40-50% cycle time reduction; Asteron's current 8-day cycle can reach 4 days in 90 days. Risk is low; success is visible.

2. **Immediate value demonstration** — INR 12 crore investment delivers INR 0.5 crore annual savings + ~20 FTE freed up + 100% audit trail (compliance win). Board sees tangible ROI in 90 days. This builds credibility for larger transformation.

3. **Sequencing story** — AP Automation is NOT the end; it's the proof point that enables the next initiatives. After 90 days of AP success, Ananya can propose:
   - **Months 4-6:** Close Redesign (leverages freed AP FTE and builds on automation momentum)
   - **Months 7-12:** Data Foundation (now that processes are optimized, standardize the data)
   - **Months 13-24:** Predictive Planning (with clean data foundation, accurate forecasting)

---

#### **90-Day Proof Point Plan**

| Phase | Week | Activity | Success Metric |
|-------|------|----------|----------------|
| **Assess** | W1-2 | Tool selection; process mapping; scope 20% of invoices | Tool selected; baseline cycle time documented |
| **Pilot** | W3-8 | Automation build; testing; soft launch | 50% of pilot invoices auto-matched; cycle time drops 30% |
| **Scale** | W9-12 | Full rollout; exceptions management; training | 80% of all invoices automated; cycle time 4 days; zero control gaps |

#### **Approval Gate (Day 90)**

- **Success = APPROVE next initiative (Close Redesign, INR 9 cr)**
- **Partial Success = EXTEND AP scope or troubleshoot exceptions**
- **Failure = PAUSE and diagnose (unlikely)**

---

### Why NOT the Others (Yet)

**Data Foundation (NOT recommended for Day 1)**
- ✅ Strategic foundation; enables all initiatives
- ❌ INR 22 crore with 18-month payoff; hard to show value in 90 days
- **Sequencing:** Fund in months 7-12 after proving AP/Close success

**Close Redesign (NOT recommended for Day 1)**
- ✅ Visible timeline improvement (14 → 8 days)
- ❌ Cross-entity change resistance; depends on process optimization
- **Sequencing:** Fund in months 4-6, leverage AP automation and freed FTE

**Predictive Planning (NOT recommended for Day 1)**
- ✅ Strategic value (decisions 18 days earlier)
- ❌ Data quality risk; garbage-in, garbage-out; needs foundation FIRST
- **Sequencing:** Fund in months 13-24 after data foundation is stable

## Step 8: Operationalize (90-Day Roadmap)

### Implementation Plan: AP Workflow Automation

#### **Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)**

| Task | Owner | Timeline | Success Criteria |
|------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Procure AP automation tool (e.g., SAP Intelligent Automation or Coupa) | CFO + CIO | W1-2 | Contract signed; licenses provisioned |
| Map current AP workflow (all 7 entities) | AP Controller + Consultant | W1-2 | Process map documented; bottlenecks identified |
| Define scope for automation (% of invoices targeted) | AP Lead + Finance Manager | W1-2 | Scope: 80% of invoices automated (exclude exceptions) |
| Establish baseline metrics | Finance Analyst | W1-2 | Current cycle time: 8 days; manual effort: 120 FTE-days/month |

#### **Phase 2: Build & Test (Weeks 3-8)**

| Task | Owner | Timeline | Success Criteria |
|------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Configure tool for 20% pilot (Asteron's largest subsidiary) | Tool Lead + IT | W3-4 | Config complete; test cases passed |
| Define 3-way match rules (invoice ↔ PO ↔ receipt) | AP Manager + Audit | W4-5 | Rules documented; test 95%+ accuracy |
| Build exception management workflow | AP Lead | W5-6 | Exceptions route to AP analyst; SLA < 1 day |
| UAT with 2 AP teams | AP Supervisors | W6-8 | 10 test cycles; 95%+ accuracy before go-live |
| Audit pre-approval | Internal Audit | W7-8 | Controls reviewed; exceptions tracked; audit trail validated |

#### **Phase 3: Scale & Optimize (Weeks 9-12)**

| Task | Owner | Timeline | Success Criteria |
|------|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Roll out to 50% of invoices across 4 entities | AP Lead + Operations | W9-10 | Pilot lessons applied; cycle time improves to 5 days |
| Train all AP staff (7 locations) | Training Lead | W9-10 | 95%+ attendance; post-training quiz > 80% |
| Monitor exceptions; refine rules | AP Lead | W10-11 | Exception rate < 5%; most resolved in < 24 hours |
| Full rollout to 80% of invoices | AP Lead | W11-12 | Cycle time: 4 days; 80% auto-match; zero control gaps |
| Measure & report results | Finance Manager | W12 | Report to Board: savings, timeline, control metrics |

---

#### **Key Metrics Dashboard (Measure Weekly)**

Track these every week to Day 90:

| Metric | Baseline | Target (D90) | Frequency |
|--------|----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Invoice cycle time** | 8 days | 4 days | Weekly |
| **% invoices auto-matched** | 0% | 80% | Weekly |
| **Manual FTE per invoice** | 0.5 hours | 0.1 hours | Weekly |
| **Exceptions per day** | N/A | < 5 | Daily |
| **Exception resolution SLA** | N/A | 95% < 24h | Weekly |
| **Audit trail completeness** | Email | 100% digital | Weekly |
| **User satisfaction** | N/A | > 4/5 | Bi-weekly |

---

#### **Risks & Mitigations**

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| **Tool doesn't match exceptions correctly** | Medium | High | Early UAT with edge cases; fallback to manual review |
| **AP staff resist change** | Medium | Medium | Early involvement; training; quick wins build adoption |
| **Data quality in invoices prevents matching** | Low | High | Pre-audit invoice data; cleanup before automation |
| **Cross-entity process differences** | High | Low | Standardize process BEFORE automation; one workflow |
| **Timeline slips in tool config** | Medium | Medium | External consultant; aggressive UAT schedule |

---

#### **Escalation Triggers (When to Alert the Board)**

- **Timeline Slip:** If pilot not complete by Week 8, escalate to CFO by Week 9
- **Control Gap:** If exceptions exceed 10%, escalate to Audit immediately
- **Adoption Issue:** If user satisfaction < 3/5 after training, escalate by Week 10
- **Cost Overrun:** If tool cost exceeds INR 12 cr budget, escalate immediately

---

#### **Success Definition (Day 90 Gate)**

**APPROVE next initiative if:**
- ✅ Invoice cycle time: 4 days (from 8)
- ✅ 80% auto-match rate
- ✅ Audit trail: 100% digital, zero control gaps
- ✅ User satisfaction: > 4/5
- ✅ Cost: Within INR 12 cr budget

**IF successful, APPROVE Close Redesign for Months 4-6 (INR 9 cr)**

---

## Summary: Asteron Decision Complete

**Recommendation:** AP Workflow Automation (INR 12 cr, 90-day proof point)

**Rationale:** Highest confidence, clearest ROI, foundation for future initiatives

**Roadmap:** AP (D1-90) → Close Redesign (M4-6) → Data Foundation (M7-12) → Planning (M13-24)

**Next Step:** Board approval; procurement begins Week 1
