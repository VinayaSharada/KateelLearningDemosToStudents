## 🎯 The Framework: NAVIGATE

**An 8-letter mnemonic for the 8-step CFO decision method:**

| Letter | Step | What | Time |
|--------|------|------|------|
| **N** | **Note** | Constraints & context | 15 min |
| **A** | **Analyze** | Baseline costs | 15 min |
| **V** | **Validate** | Option benefits | 20 min |
| **I** | **Inspect** | 90-day outcomes | 20 min |
| **G** | **Gauge** | Trade-offs | 20 min |
| **A** | **Assess** | Risks | 20 min |
| **T** | **Take** | Decision | 15 min |
| **E** | **Execute** | Implementation plan | 30 min |

**Total Time:** 2.5 hours to solve a complex decision

### **INSTRUCTOR_FACILITATION_NOTES.md**

**Before Class (3 hours prep):**
- Read & analyze case fully
- Prepare worked example
- Annotate teaching notes
- Print materials

**Classroom Setup:**
- Physical space (tables, whiteboard, projector)
- Materials (handouts, templates)
- Breakout rooms (if large class)

**Teaching Sequence (45-90 min walkthrough):**
- Opening (15 min): Context & energy
- Method review (20 min): Refresh the 8 steps
- Case intro (15 min): Establish business context
- Walkthrough (45 min): Model the thinking process step-by-step
- Group exercise (90 min): Students do analysis
- Presentations (30-45 min): Groups present & compare
- Closure (15 min): Big picture & action

**Common Mistakes (& How to Handle):**
1. "We should do everything" → Force prioritization
2. "Choose the cheapest option" → Balance with other criteria
3. "We can't decide without perfect info" → Make decisions with incomplete data
4. "Our recommendation differs from the example" → Validate multiple approaches
5. "We didn't finish the 8 steps" → Push to complete the work

**Assessment:**
- Group work: 60% (clear steps, quantified analysis, defensible recommendation, professional memo)
- Participation: 40% (engagement, questions, listening)

## ✨ The NAVIGATE Advantage

**Compared to Gut-Feel Decisions:**
- ✅ Transparent reasoning (everyone sees the logic)
- ✅ Quantified impact (numbers, not opinions)
- ✅ Risk-aware (mitigations identified)
- ✅ Defensible (you can explain every choice)
- ✅ Repeatable (use for all future decisions)

**Compared to Analysis Paralysis:**
- ✅ Structured (not endless debate)
- ✅ Time-boxed (2.5 hours max)
- ✅ Decision-focused (not just data gathering)
- ✅ Action-oriented (ends with implementation plan)
