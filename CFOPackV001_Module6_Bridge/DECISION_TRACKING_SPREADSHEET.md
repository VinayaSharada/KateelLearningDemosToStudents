# Decision-Tracking Spreadsheet Template

**Track and compare recommendations across all groups**

---

## How to Use This Template

This spreadsheet helps instructors:
1. **Track all recommendations** across groups and cases
2. **Compare approaches** to the same case
3. **Identify patterns** (which options are chosen most?)
4. **Grade consistency** (clear reasoning vs. unclear)
5. **Build discussion** (show different valid approaches)

**Format:** CSV-compatible table (can copy to Excel/Google Sheets)

---

# Comparison Matrix: Same Case, Different Recommendations

## Case 1: Asteron AP (4 Groups, 3 Different Recommendations)

| Aspect | Group A Redesign | Group B RPA | Group C Redesign | Group D ERP |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Cost** | INR 14 cr | INR 12 cr | INR 14 cr | INR 35 cr |
| **Day 90 Proof Point** | 40% faster, zero control gaps | 80% auto-match, but exceptions pile up | 40% faster, zero control gaps | Slower to show value |
| **Risk Assessment** | Low adoption risk | Medium adoption risk | Low adoption risk | High implementation risk |
| **Sequencing** | Foundation for AR automation | Doesn't enable next initiatives | Foundation for AR automation | Foundation for everything |
| **Primary Rationale** | Control first, speed second | Speed first, control later | Control first, speed second | Strategic foundation |
| **Grade** | 92 | 85 | 90 | 93 |

### Discussion Points from This Comparison

**1. "Why did Group B choose pure RPA?"**
- Cost focus (INR 12 vs. INR 14 crore)
- Speed orientation (80% auto-match in 90 days)
- Assumed control risk could be managed later

**2. "Why did Group D choose ERP?"**
- Strategic thinking (not just AP)
- Understood foundation matters
- Willing to accept longer ROI timeline

**3. "What would a real CFO choose?"**
- Depends on Board priorities:
  - Cost-conscious? → Group B
  - Control-focused? → Group A or C
  - Long-term thinker? → Group D

**4. "Is Group B wrong?"**
- Not wrong, just different priorities
- Control risk needs mitigation strategy (not mentioned in memo)
- Could work if: Asteron is a startup (speed > control) or has strong post-automation controls plan

# How to Present Results Back to Class

**Option A: Short Debrief (10 min)**

> "Here's how you all approached Case 1:
> 
> Most of you recommended Redesign + Selective Automation (control-focused).  
> One group recommended Pure RPA (speed-focused).  
> One group recommended full ERP (strategic).
> 
> All three are defensible. The difference is priorities:
> - Group A/C: Control first
> - Group B: Speed first
> - Group D: Foundation first
> 
> In reality, the Board's priorities determine which is right. Any questions?"

**Option B: Detailed Discussion (30 min)**

Present the comparison matrix. Discuss:
- Why did groups make different choices?
- Which recommendation is most aligned with the case context?
- What would you recommend if you were the CFO?

**Option C: Individual Feedback (15 min per group)**

One-on-one with each group:
- "Your reasoning on control was strong. Next time, add more detail on risk mitigation."
- "I like your cost focus. Also consider: Could this compromise control?"

# Using This Data for Learning

### **1. Identify Struggling Groups**

**Groups with grades < 80:**
- Follow up with individual feedback
- Assign additional practice (another case)
- Check if they understand the 8-step method

### **2. Identify Outlier Recommendations**

**Example:** All groups recommend Option A except Group X recommends Option B
- Ask Group X: "Walk me through your reasoning"
- If sound: celebrate independent thinking
- If flawed: teaching opportunity

### **3. Identify Missing Steps**

**Example:** 50% of groups skipped Step 6 (risk mitigation)
- Next case, emphasize Step 6
- Show how risks can undermine recommendations
- Require risk mitigation in memo

### **4. Build Class Discussions**

**Compare recommendations side-by-side:**
- "Here's why Group A chose Redesign. Here's why Group D chose ERP. Which would YOU choose and why?"
- "What assumption is different between these two groups?"

### **5. Track Progress Over Cases**

**Look at grades over time:**
- Case 1 average: 90
- Case 2 average: (fill in)
- Case 3 average: (fill in)

If improving: method is sticking  
If declining: intervention needed  
If flat: consider different teaching approach
