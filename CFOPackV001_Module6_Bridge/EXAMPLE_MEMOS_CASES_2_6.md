# CASE 2: Asteron AP Automation

## MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD

**FROM:** Vikram Sharma, Chief Financial Officer  
**TO:** Finance & Investment Committee  
**DATE:** Month 2026  
**RE:** AP Process Transformation Initiative  
**BUDGET REQUESTED:** INR 14 crore | **TIMELINE:** 120 days

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Recommend:** Deep process redesign + selective RPA automation (not pure automation)

**Why:** Asteron's AP function has a **control problem, not just a speed problem.** Simply automating today's email-based 3-way match will lock in control gaps. We need to redesign the process FIRST, then automate the clean version.

**Investment:** INR 14 crore (vs. INR 12 cr for pure RPA)  
**Day 120 Benefit:** 40% faster cycle, 50% fewer exceptions, audit trail 100% digital, zero control gaps

---

### 1. SITUATION: The AP Vulnerability

Current AP process costs us **INR 1.2 crore/year** in inefficiency and risk:

| Cost | Amount | Driver |
|------|--------|--------|
| **Manual matching time** | INR 0.4 cr | 2 FTE doing exception investigation |
| **Accrual errors** | INR 0.3 cr | Invoice matches to wrong PO; rework required |
| **Compliance risk** | INR 0.3 cr | Email approvals; no audit trail; regulatory risk |
| **Reconciliation delays** | INR 0.2 cr | 3-4 days to reconcile AP to GL |

**The Hidden Issue:** Our AP team is manually working around control gaps. If we automate the current process, we'll automate those gaps.

---

### 2. ANALYSIS: Why Pure Automation Fails Here

Four approaches were considered:

| Approach | Cost | Control | Day 120 Value | Risk |
|----------|------|---------|---------------|------|
| **Pure RPA** (automate as-is) | INR 12 cr | Medium | High | **Control gaps locked in** |
| **Redesign + Selective Automation** | INR 14 cr | High | Medium | Low |
| **Full ERP Upgrade** | INR 35 cr | Highest | Lowest | Integration complexity |
| **Hybrid with Controls Added Later** | INR 13 cr | Low initially | Medium | High (retrofit controls = rework) |

**Key Insight:** If we automate today's process (email approvals, exception workarounds), we're solving a speed problem while creating a control problem. RPA will accelerate bad processes.

---

### 3. RECOMMENDATION: Redesign + Selective Automation

**Invest INR 14 crore in a phased approach:**

**Phase 1: Process Redesign (Weeks 1-4, INR 3 cr)**
- Streamline 3-way match rules (eliminate edge cases)
- Build digital approval workflow (replace email)
- Design exception triage (not all exceptions are urgent)
- Implement electronic PO + Invoice matching (not manual lookup)

**Phase 2: Selective Automation (Weeks 5-8, INR 8 cr)**
- RPA on 60% of invoices (clean path, high-match rules)
- Leave 40% as manual-but-controlled (exceptions, edge cases)
- Digital approval routing for all (automated or manual)
- Exception triage to right owner (not a black hole)

**Phase 3: Measure & Optimize (Weeks 9-12, INR 3 cr)**
- Monitor exception rates by reason
- Optimize rules based on real data
- Train team on new process
- Validate control strength

---

### 4. WHY NOT PURE RPA?

Pure RPA is tempting (cheaper, faster) but wrong for Asteron:

**Example:** Today's process = "If invoice matches PO and receipt, approve. Otherwise, send email."

With pure RPA:
- ✅ 80% of invoices now auto-approve (faster)
- ❌ But 20% of exceptions go to an "exception queue" with no owner
- ❌ Exception backlogs grow
- ❌ Audit has 8 questions about why exceptions pile up
- ❌ We've automated the speed problem but worsened the control problem

With redesign + selective automation:
- ✅ Process is clean FIRST
- ✅ Then we automate the clean parts
- ✅ Exceptions are owned and tracked
- ✅ Audit has one dashboard

---

### 5. CONTROL & RISK MITIGATION

**Biggest Risk:** Process redesign creates adoption resistance

**Mitigation Strategy:**
- Communicate benefit clearly ("You'll have 50% less exception work")
- Involve AP team in redesign (not IT-driven)
- Test with pilot (one entity) before full rollout
- Celebrate first wins publicly

**Audit Perspective:** This approach STRENGTHENS audit position
- Exception management is transparent
- Approval authority is preserved (not delegated to RPA)
- Control gaps are eliminated, not automated

---

### 6. TIMELINE: 120-Day Roadmap

| Phase | Weeks | Deliverable | Success Metric |
|-------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Design** | 1-4 | New AP process map; approval workflow design; rule library | Design approved by audit; team comfortable |
| **Build** | 5-8 | RPA configured; digital workflow live; exceptions routed | 60% of invoices auto-matching; exceptions tracked |
| **Test** | 9-10 | UAT with 1 entity; control validation; training | 95% match accuracy; exceptions owned; audit passes |
| **Deploy** | 11-12 | Full rollout to all 7 entities | 3-day cycle time; 0 control gaps; team trained |

**Day 120 Gate Criteria:**
- ✅ 60% of invoices auto-matched
- ✅ Cycle time: 3 days (from 8)
- ✅ Exceptions: < 5/day, all owned
- ✅ Audit trail: 100% digital
- ✅ Control assessment: PASS (zero gaps)

---

### 7. FINANCIAL IMPACT

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Investment** | INR 14 crore |
| **Year 1 Savings** | INR 0.8 crore (FTE + rework reduction) |
| **Year 2+ Savings** | INR 1.2 crore (efficiency + compliance benefit) |
| **Payback Period** | 15 months |
| **Strategic Value** | Clean process enables future automation (collections, payables planning) |

---

### 8. SEQUENCING: What's Next?

**Day 120+:** If successful, consider:
- Close Process Redesign (same approach: redesign + automate)
- Collections Optimization (acceleration of collections cycle)
- Payables Planning (predictive payables forecasting)

---

# CASE 3: Asteron GenAI for Financial Planning & Analysis

## MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD

**FROM:** Priya Desai, CFO  
**TO:** Board of Directors  
**DATE:** Month 2026  
**RE:** AI-Powered FPA Pilot: Accelerating Planning Cycle  
**BUDGET REQUESTED:** INR 8 crore | **TIMELINE:** 120 days

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Recommend:** GenAI-assisted FPA (not full automation; human-led with AI support)

**Why:** Our FPA team spends 60% of month 2 on routine analysis ("What drove variance?", "Model this scenario"). GenAI can handle these routine prompts, freeing FPA to focus on strategic interpretation. But we must maintain human judgment at the point of CFO communication.

**Investment:** INR 8 crore (lower cost because human remains central)  
**Day 120 Benefit:** Forecast ready Day 15 (vs. Day 18); CFO monthly narrative generated in 2 hours (vs. 6 hours of meetings)

---

### 1. SITUATION: The FPA Bottleneck

FPA cycle takes **18 days after month-end.** Breakdown:

| Task | Days | Owner | Value |
|------|------|-------|-------|
| Data consolidation & validation | 2-3 | Accounting + FPA | Mandatory |
| Variance analysis ("Why are we off?") | 3-4 | FPA (time-consuming) | **Routine, repetitive** |
| Scenario modeling ("What if...?") | 3-4 | FPA | **Routine, repetitive** |
| Strategic narrative drafting | 2-3 | FPA + CFO | **High-value, judgment-based** |
| CFO review & revision | 3-4 | CFO | Mandatory |
| Final commentary & distribution | 1-2 | CFO | Mandatory |

**The Problem:** Months 2 days take 5-8 days of pure data work (variance + scenarios), delaying strategic narrative that takes CFO 3 days.

**The Opportunity:** GenAI can do variance analysis and scenario modeling in 2-3 hours (not 6-7 days), freeing FPA to focus on interpretation.

---

### 2. ANALYSIS: AI vs. Manual vs. Hybrid

Three approaches evaluated:

| Approach | Cost | Day 15 Feasible? | CFO Confidence | Control Risk |
|----------|------|------------------|-----------------|------------|
| **Status Quo** (all manual) | INR 0 | No (18 days) | High | Low |
| **Full AI Automation** | INR 6 cr | Yes, but risky | Low (AI writes narrative) | High (loses audit trail) |
| **Hybrid: AI-Assisted with Human Lead** | INR 8 cr | Yes, safely | High (FPA reviews all) | Low (humans decide) |

**Why Hybrid Wins:**
- GenAI handles routine analysis (variance, scenarios) — this is 70% of FPA time
- FPA interprets outputs and adds judgment — this is 30% of time, but 100% of value
- CFO focuses on strategic narrative, not data wrangling

---

### 3. RECOMMENDATION: Hybrid GenAI-Assisted FPA

**Invest INR 8 crore to build three things:**

**1. Variance Automation (INR 2 cr)**
- Prompt template: "Here's the budget. Here's actual. What's different?"
- GenAI generates variance analysis with source line items
- FPA reviews, validates, interprets
- Output: Variance table + early warnings

**2. Scenario Modeling (INR 3 cr)**
- Prompt template: "Model scenario: Revenue +5%, Costs +2%"
- GenAI generates spreadsheet + narrative
- FPA stress-tests assumptions
- Output: 3-4 scenarios + CFO options

**3. Executive Narrative (INR 3 cr)**
- Prompt template: "Synthesize variances, scenarios, and market signals into CFO narrative"
- GenAI drafts using variance + scenario outputs
- CFO edits & adds strategic perspective
- Output: 2-page CFO commentary ready for Board

---

### 4. CONTROL: THE CFO REMAINS IN CHARGE

This is NOT "AI writes the Board commentary." It's "AI does the data work, CFO writes the narrative."

**Day 15 FPA Timeline (with GenAI):**

| Day | Task | Owner |
|-----|------|-------|
| D1-2 | Data consolidation | Accounting |
| D2-3 | GenAI: Variance analysis runs | Automated |
| D3-4 | FPA: Review variance, validate, interpret | FPA lead |
| D4-5 | GenAI: Scenario modeling runs (3-4 scenarios) | Automated |
| D5-7 | FPA: Stress-test scenarios; choose strategic options | FPA lead |
| D7-8 | GenAI: Draft CFO narrative (using variance + scenario data) | Automated |
| D8-12 | CFO: Edit narrative; add strategic interpretation; prepare Board slides | CFO |
| D13-15 | Final review; print & distribute | Communications |

**Day 15 Ready:** Yes. And CFO has added judgment at every step.

---

### 5. WHAT ABOUT AUDIT & TRACEABILITY?

**Audit concern:** "GenAI can hallucinate. How do we validate?"

**Answer:** All GenAI outputs are source-linked.

Example: GenAI says "Revenue up 8% due to Q2 bookings surge"
- ✅ Variance report hyperlinks to source GL lines
- ✅ Supporting data is provided (actual Q2 booking numbers)
- ✅ FPA validates the interpretation
- ✅ CFO can explain to audit exactly what happened

**Audit trail:** Prompts + inputs + outputs + FPA/CFO edits = full lineage

---

### 6. RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| **GenAI hallucinates a number** | Medium | High | Source-link all outputs; FPA validates before CFO sees |
| **CFO loses control to AI narrative** | Low | High | CFO edits all outputs; AI is support, not author |
| **Team resists; thinks job is threatened** | Medium | Low | Position as "You'll focus on interpretation, not data wrangling" |
| **Prompt quality degrades over time** | Low | Medium | Document prompts; quarterly review with GenAI vendor |

---

### 7. TIMELINE: 120-Day Rollout

| Phase | Weeks | Deliverable | Success Metric |
|-------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Design** | 1-3 | Prompt templates; validation rules; audit trail design | Audit approves approach |
| **Build** | 4-6 | GenAI integration; data connections; draft automation | Prompts tested; outputs validated |
| **Pilot** | 7-10 | Month-end close with GenAI (parallel with manual) | GenAI variance = manual variance; no hallucinations |
| **Deploy** | 11-12 | Full GenAI FPA on Nov/Dec close | Day 15 ready; CFO satisfied with narrative |

**Day 120 Gate:**
- ✅ Variance analysis ready by Day 3 (automated)
- ✅ Scenarios ready by Day 7 (automated)
- ✅ CFO narrative ready by Day 15 (CFO-led)
- ✅ Audit validation: zero hallucinations, full source lineage
- ✅ Team satisfaction: FPA loves fewer data tasks

---

### 8. FINANCIAL IMPACT

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Investment** | INR 8 crore |
| **Year 1 Savings** | INR 0.4 crore (FTE time reduction: 3 days/month × 12) |
| **Year 2+ Savings** | INR 0.6 crore (faster planning = faster decisions = better outcomes) |
| **Payback Period** | 14 months |
| **Strategic Value** | 18-day cycle → 15-day cycle; FPA freed for strategic analysis |

---

### 9. SEQUENCING

Post-Day 120:
- Rolling forecast (weekly reforecasting instead of monthly)
- Real-time variance alerts (not Month+18 analysis)
- Predictive planning (AI model next quarter vs. react to current)

---

**Respectfully submitted,**

Priya Desai  
Chief Financial Officer

# CASE 4: Vistara Technology Selection

## MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD

**FROM:** Ramesh Kumar, Chief Financial Officer  
**TO:** Strategy & Technology Committee  
**DATE:** Month 2026  
**RE:** Finance Technology Roadmap: Cloud ERP + Modern Stack  
**BUDGET REQUESTED:** INR 65 crore (3-year program) | **TIMELINE:** Year 1: INR 25 cr proof point

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Recommend:** Cloud ERP (Oracle Cloud or SAP S/4HANA) + Modern Finance Stack (GenAI, RPA, analytics)

**Why:** Vistara's legacy ERP is no longer serving the business. System limitations drive manual workarounds in AP, AR, close. A cloud ERP gives us a single system, clean data, and built-in automation. Year 1 investment proves ROI in faster close and unified reporting.

**Investment:** INR 25 crore (Year 1 proof point)  
**Day 120 Benefit:** Single chart of accounts; one AP system; daily close capability (vs. 5-day monthly close)

---

### 1. SITUATION: Legacy System Constraints

Vistara's 3 legacy ERPs cost us **INR 2.5 crore/year** in inefficiency:

| System Issue | Impact | Cost |
|--------------|--------|------|
| **Three separate AP systems** | No consolidated payables view; manual reconciliation | INR 0.8 cr |
| **No automated close** | 5 days of manual close work across entities | INR 0.7 cr |
| **12 chart-of-account mappings** | Slow reporting; reconciliation; analyst time | INR 0.6 cr |
| **Poor data quality** | Manual data entry; rework; audit queries | INR 0.4 cr |

**The Trap:** Every improvement we try (automation, analytics, AI) fails because data is fragmented across 3 systems.

---

### 2. ANALYSIS: Four Paths Forward

| Path | Cost | Year 1 Value | Risk | Time to Modern |
|------|------|-------------|------|-----------------|
| **Stay & Patch** | INR 0 | INR 0 | Falling further behind | Never |
| **Best-of-Breed (stay legacy + add tools)** | INR 15 cr | Marginal | Still fragmented data | 5+ years |
| **Cloud ERP + selective tools** | INR 25 cr | INR 1.2 cr (payback) | Integration complexity | 2 years |
| **Full replacement (rip & replace)** | INR 40 cr | INR 1.5 cr | Disruption risk | 18 months |

**Why Cloud ERP Wins:** 
- Single source of truth (fixes fragmentation)
- Built-in automation (close, AP, AR)
- Modern data (enables analytics/AI)
- Reasonable risk profile (phased implementation)

---

### 3. RECOMMENDATION: Cloud ERP Program (3-Year Roadmap)

**Year 1 Proof Point (INR 25 crore):**

**Phase 1: Implement Core (Months 1-4)**
- Deploy Cloud ERP (Oracle Cloud Finance or SAP S/4)
- Migrate AP from 3 systems into one
- Establish single chart of accounts
- Day 1: One payables system live

**Phase 2: Optimize (Months 5-8)**
- Automate AP matching (RPA or native automation)
- Automate close process (nightly GL consolidation)
- Build unified reporting

**Phase 3: Prove Value (Months 9-12)**
- Deliver metrics: close in 1 day (vs. 5), one AP system, zero manual reconciliation
- Measure: FTE time saved, error rate, cycle time
- Plan Year 2 (AR, planning, supply chain)

---

### 4. CONTROL & RISK MANAGEMENT

**Biggest Risk:** Implementation complexity

**Mitigation:**
- Hire Big 4 implementation partner (part of INR 25 cr)
- Phased go-live (AP first, close second, AR third)
- Parallel run for 2 weeks (old + new system)
- Daily steering committee (CFO + CIO + vendor)

**Audit Perspective:**
- Cloud ERP has stronger built-in controls than legacy systems
- System audit trails are native (not added later)
- Segregation of duties is configurable (not manual)

---

### 5. TIMELINE: Year 1 Proof Point

| Month | Deliverable | Success Criteria |
|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| M1-2 | ERP selection & contract; team mobilization | Vendor selected; team onboarded |
| M3-4 | System configuration; AP rules; GL mapping | Config complete; UAT starts |
| M5-6 | UAT & readiness; parallel run setup | 95%+ test pass; parallel run planned |
| M7-8 | Go-live AP; parallel run (2 weeks); cutover | Zero critical issues; data integrity validated |
| M9-10 | Close process automation; reporting build | First automated close; metrics tracked |
| M11-12 | Stabilize & measure; plan Year 2 | Proof point validated; Board decision on Year 2 |

**Day 120 Gate (Month 4):**
- ✅ ERP selected and contracted
- ✅ Team mobilized and trained
- ✅ Configuration complete
- ✅ Go-live date locked (Month 7)

**Day 365 Gate (Month 12):**
- ✅ AP consolidated into one system
- ✅ Close cycle: 1 day (vs. 5)
- ✅ Reporting: unified view across all entities
- ✅ Cost: INR 25 cr spent
- ✅ Year 2 ROI validated → Approve Year 2 (INR 20 cr for AR, planning)

---

### 6. FINANCIAL IMPACT

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Year 1 Investment** | INR 25 crore |
| **Year 1 Savings** | INR 0.6 crore (close FTE, AP FTE, reconciliation) |
| **Year 2-3 Savings** | INR 1.8 crore/year (AR, planning, analytics) |
| **3-Year Total Investment** | INR 65 crore |
| **3-Year Total Savings** | INR 5+ crore |
| **Payback Period** | Year 3, Month 4 |
| **Strategic Value** | Modern finance foundation for 10+ years |

---

### 7. SEQUENCING: 3-Year Vision

**Year 1:** AP consolidation + close automation  
**Year 2:** AR optimization + forecasting automation  
**Year 3:** Supply chain finance + advanced analytics

# CASE 5: Asteron CFO-CIO Portfolio

## MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD

**FROM:** Ananya Rao, CFO + Ramesh Sinha, CIO  
**TO:** Board of Directors  
**DATE:** Month 2026  
**RE:** Finance-IT Partnership: Digital Finance Roadmap  
**BUDGET REQUESTED:** INR 120 crore (3 years) | **TIMELINE:** Quarterly gates

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Recommend:** CFO-CIO co-led digital finance program with quarterly business gates

**Why:** Previous attempts to digitize finance failed because CFO and CIO had different success metrics. CFO wants business impact (faster close, better decisions). CIO wants system stability and tech debt reduction. This proposal aligns both through:
- Shared governance (CFO-CIO co-lead)
- Business gates (CFO decides what's valuable)
- Tech gates (CIO ensures quality)
- Portfolio sequencing (balance quick wins + foundation work)

**Investment:** INR 120 crore over 3 years  
**Day 120 Benefit:** Portfolio roadmap approved; governance established; Year 1 budget allocated

---

### 1. SITUATION: The CFO-CIO Tension

Asteron has spent INR 40 crore on "digital finance" projects over 3 years:
- ✅ SAP upgraded (tech debt resolved)
- ❌ But close still takes 14 days (no process improvement)
- ✅ Analytics platform built (modern tech stack)
- ❌ But adoption is 20% (users prefer Excel)
- ✅ RPA evaluated (best practices learned)
- ❌ But no live automations (quality concerns)

**The Problem:** Finance and IT optimized for different things.
- IT: "Reduce tech debt, improve system stability, enterprise standards"
- CFO: "Faster close, fewer errors, better decisions"

These don't have to conflict, but they were managed separately. Result: INR 40 cr spent, minimal CFO value.

---

### 2. ANALYSIS: What Went Wrong (and How to Fix It)

**Previous Model: Siloed Decisions**

```
CFO → "We need AP automation"  →  IT → "We need to stabilize SAP first"
         ↓                                ↓
    "Finance is impatient"           "Finance is unrealistic"
         ↓                                ↓
    Project delayed 6+ months       Tech debt never addressed
         ↓
    Neither side happy
```

**Proposed Model: Shared Governance**

```
         CFO + CIO (Co-Leads)
              ↓
    Define Portfolio (3-year vision)
         ↓
    Quarterly Business Gate
    (Is this valuable to CFO?)
         ↓
    Quarterly Tech Gate
    (Is this technically sound?)
         ↓
    If both: Approved & funded
    If conflict: Resolved at Exec level
```

---

### 3. RECOMMENDATION: CFO-CIO Partnership Program

**3-Year Portfolio (INR 120 crore):**

**Year 1 (INR 50 cr): Foundation**
- Stabilize SAP (tech debt) — INR 15 cr
- Build data foundation — INR 15 cr
- Quick wins (AP automation, close redesign) — INR 20 cr
- Shared governance + team capability — INR 5 cr

**Year 2 (INR 40 cr): Scale**
- AR optimization — INR 15 cr
- Planning platform — INR 15 cr
- Analytics on clean data — INR 10 cr

**Year 3 (INR 30 cr): Advanced**
- Predictive analytics — INR 12 cr
- AI-driven decision support — INR 10 cr
- Supply chain finance — INR 8 cr

---

### 4. GOVERNANCE: QUARTERLY GATES

**Every 3 months, CFO + CIO + Board review:**

| Gate | CFO Questions | CIO Questions | Go/No-Go |
|------|---------------|---------------|----------|
| **Business Value** | Is this valuable? Will it improve close/decisions? | — | CFO decides |
| **Technical Feasibility** | — | Is this technically sound? Do we have capacity? | CIO decides |
| **Financial** | Is ROI clear? Is timeline realistic? | Is cost estimate accurate? | Joint decision |
| **Risk** | Does this strengthen control? | Does this impact system stability? | Joint decision |

**If disagreement:** Escalate to Exec Committee (resolve at CEO level)

**Example:** "Finance wants AI-driven close (Year 2). IT says data foundation isn't ready."
- **Gate 1 (Business):** CFO: "Yes, this creates value"
- **Gate 2 (Tech):** CIO: "Not feasible until Q2 Year 2"
- **Resolution:** Delayed 6 months; both accept trade-off

---

### 5. TEAM STRUCTURE & CAPABILITY

**New Shared Organization:**

```
CFO + CIO (Co-Executive Sponsors)
    ↓
Digital Finance Steering Committee
├─ Finance SVP (process owner)
├─ IT SVP (technology owner)
├─ Controller (close owner)
├─ Business Analyst (translator)
└─ Program Manager (coordination)

    ↓
Three Workstreams
├─ Foundation (data, systems stability)
├─ Quick Wins (AP, close, AR)
└─ Advanced (analytics, planning, AI)
```

**Critical:** Business analyst who speaks both languages (finance + tech)

---

### 6. SUCCESS CRITERIA

**Year 1 (INR 50 cr):**
- ✅ Close reduced from 14 days to 7 days
- ✅ AP FTE reduced 20%
- ✅ Data foundation supporting 2+ analytics initiatives
- ✅ SAP stability: 99.5% uptime
- ✅ CFO-CIO aligned on Year 2 priorities

**Year 2 (INR 40 cr):**
- ✅ Close reduced to 3 days
- ✅ AR improved 30%
- ✅ Planning cycle reduced to 10 days
- ✅ Positive ROI on Year 1 + Year 2 combined

**Year 3 (INR 30 cr):**
- ✅ Predictive analytics live (forecast accuracy > 90%)
- ✅ Total CFO-valued savings > INR 3 cr/year
- ✅ Team capable of next wave of innovation

---

### 7. FINANCIAL IMPACT

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **3-Year Investment** | INR 120 crore |
| **Year 1 Savings** | INR 0.4 crore |
| **Year 2 Savings** | INR 0.8 crore |
| **Year 3 Savings** | INR 1.2 crore |
| **Year 4+ Run-Rate** | INR 1.5+ crore/year |
| **Payback Period** | Year 4 (cumulative) |

---

### 8. WHY THIS WORKS

✅ **Clear governance:** Both CFO and CIO have veto; resolves escalation properly  
✅ **Shared metrics:** Not competing (tech debt vs. business value), aligning (both matter)  
✅ **Quarterly gates:** Short feedback loops; if Year 1 goes wrong, adjust Year 2  
✅ **Portfolio approach:** Balance quick wins + foundation (not one or the other)  
✅ **Capability:** Shared team; business analyst bridges gap  

---

**Respectfully submitted,**

# CASE 6: Vistara Board Roadmap

## MEMORANDUM TO THE BOARD

**FROM:** Rakesh Iyer, Chief Financial Officer  
**TO:** Board of Directors  
**DATE:** Month 2026  
**RE:** Finance Digital Transformation: 3-Year Board Roadmap  
**BUDGET REQUESTED:** INR 85 crore | **TIMELINE:** 36 months with quarterly gates

---

### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Recommend:** Integrated 3-year transformation combining ERP modernization, process automation, and AI adoption

**Why:** Vistara's finance function is constrained by legacy systems, fragmented processes, and limited analytics. A coordinated 3-year program addresses all three simultaneously, creating a modern finance organization capable of real-time decision-making.

**Year 1 Focus:** Foundation (ERP + data + quick wins)  
**Year 2 Focus:** Scale (automation + analytics)  
**Year 3 Focus:** Optimize (AI + strategic analytics)

**Investment:** INR 85 crore (over 3 years)  
**Board Gate:** Quarterly; any gate can halt program if ROI not tracking

---

### 1. SITUATION: Strategic Imperative

Vistara's Board asked: "Why does finance take 18 days for month-end close when competitors close in 3 days?"

**Root Causes:**
1. **System fragmentation:** 3 ERP systems, 8 general ledgers = manual consolidation
2. **Process immaturity:** Close is manual, not designed for speed
3. **Analytics gap:** CFO has numbers but limited insight ("Why?" questions take 2 weeks to answer)

**Business Consequence:** Decision cycles are slow. Market opportunities are missed while finance gathers data.

---

### 2. ANALYSIS: Three-Year Program vs. Incremental

| Approach | Cost | 3-Year Vision | Risk |
|----------|------|---------------|------|
| **Incremental** (fix one thing at a time) | INR 60 cr | Fragmented; still 3 systems; slow progress | High (takes 7 years) |
| **Integrated 3-Year Program** | INR 85 cr | Modern; one system; real-time decisions | Medium (ambitious but coordinated) |
| **Full Rip & Replace** | INR 120 cr | Complete modern stack | High (disruption) |

**Why 3-Year Integrated Wins:**
- Coordinated initiatives reduce duplication (don't build analytics on fragmented data)
- Shared team reduces cost (don't build separate teams for each initiative)
- Quarterly gates allow flexibility (if Year 1 plan changes, Year 2-3 adjust)

---

### 3. RECOMMENDATION: 3-Year Integrated Roadmap

### **Year 1: FOUNDATION (INR 35 crore)**

**Goals:**
- [ ] Single finance system live (consolidate 3 ERP into 1)
- [ ] Unified data foundation (one GL, one chart of accounts)
- [ ] Quick wins (AP, close, reporting)

### 7. SEQUENCING LOGIC (Why This Order?)

**Why ERP → Data Foundation → Analytics?**

```
Legacy Systems                Cloud ERP                Modern Finance
(Today)                       (Year 1)                 (Year 3)

3 ERP systems      ───→      1 ERP system      ───→   1 system
8 general ledgers            1 general ledger          Unified data
Manual close                 Automated close           Predictive
Month+18 reporting           Month+5 reporting         Real-time insights

Each layer enables the next.
Can't build clean analytics on fragmented data.
Can't move fast without modern processes.
```

**Why Not Start with Analytics?**

"Let's skip the ERP and build analytics directly on legacy systems"

❌ Won't work because:
- Data is fragmented (8 GLs can't be reconciled to one P&L)
- Data quality is poor (legacy systems have 20 years of technical debt)
- Analysts spend time reconciling, not analyzing
