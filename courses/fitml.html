Learning outcomes

What this course pack builds

Core outcomes

  • Formulate and evaluate demographic parity, disparate impact, and equal opportunity fairness metrics.
  • Design constrained in-processing optimization and causal counterfactual fairness DAGs.
  • Explain local and global model predictions using cooperative game-theoretic Shapley values (SHAP) and LIME.
  • Audit vision model vulnerability against adversarial perturbations (FGSM/PGD) and implement robust defenses.

Audience fit

Machine learning engineers, AI product managers, quantitative risk officers, algorithmic auditors, and data science students seeking rigorous mathematical and code-level mastery of trustworthy AI.

Curated public packClassroom ready

Faculty prompt

Have students compute both fairness metrics and feature attributions before and after applying debiasing interventions, and defend whether the change produced an ethically defensible and regulatory-compliant model.

Algorithmic Bias Detection & Pre-Processing

Intermediate

Measure algorithmic bias across protected attributes using disparate impact and equalized odds, followed by instance reweighting and disparate impact removal pre-processing on COMPAS and Census data.

Teacher cue: Compute disparate impact and statistical parity on COMPAS and Adult Income datasets, then apply reweighting and disparate impact removal pre-processing to evaluate the fairness-accuracy trade-off.

Feature Attribution (LIME & SHAP)

Intermediate

Audit black-box models using Shapley values (SHAP) and local surrogate models (LIME) to deliver stakeholder-ready prediction attributions and detect spurious correlations.

Teacher cue: Generate local LIME explanations and Tree SHAP force plots for individual predictions; compare local fidelity against global feature importance.

Vision XAI & Adversarial Robustness

Advanced

Inspect vision neural networks using Grad-CAM saliency maps and test vulnerability against FGSM and PGD adversarial evasion attacks with robust min-max training defense.
