Learning outcomes

What this course pack builds

Core outcomes

  • Interpret financial, cyber, and model-risk signals in a structured way.
  • Compare baseline and stress outcomes rather than isolated single numbers.
  • Connect risk metrics to controls, limits, and escalation actions.
  • Recognize where polished outputs can still hide uncertainty or blind spots.

Contagion Model

All Levels

One bank fails. Does it crash the whole system? Model how losses propagate through interconnected institutions. See where your exposure lies and what breaks first. Learn why regulators fear systemic risk.

Teacher cue: Ask: "Which institution, if it failed, would trigger the biggest cascade? Why?" This teaches systemic thinking.

Counter Party Risk

Intermediate

Your bank lends to a seemingly healthy company. Their major customer just announced layoffs. How deep is your exposure? Monitor supply chain concentration and exposure triggers that predict defaults before ratings agencies do.

Teacher cue: Ask: "Which one customer could bring them down? What's your exposure there?" This teaches forward-looking risk.

AI Risk Calculator

Intermediate

AI model says "approve this loan." But can't explain why. Do you trust it? When does explainability matter more than accuracy? Learn when risk officers demand to understand the model before acting on it.

Teacher cue: Challenge: "Would you stake your job on unexplained predictions? Why or why not?" This reveals governance boundaries.

Counterparty Risk

Intermediate

You have $500M exposure to a counterparty. Their credit rating just dropped. What do you do? Reduce exposure? Demand more collateral? Learn when concentration becomes unacceptable and how to monitor credit signals that predict defaults.

Teacher cue: Ask: "At what exposure level would you demand action? Who decides?" This teaches risk governance.

Risk Parity Portfolio

Advanced

Stocks rally; bonds fall. Balance portfolio so each asset contributes equal risk, not equal dollars. Why equal-weight 60/40 fails. See how risk contributions compound and why diversification requires rethinking weights.

Teacher cue: Ask: "Why not just 60/40 stocks/bonds? What's the benefit of equal risk contribution?" This teaches portfolio thinking beyond traditional allocation.

VaR Calculator

Advanced

How much can you lose in a bad market day? 1-in-100 scenario or 1-in-1000? Learn tail risk vs. average case. See why VaR is useful but incomplete—it measures risk but not the worst-case beyond it.

Teacher cue: Ask: "Are you optimizing for expected loss or worst-case? What's your risk tolerance?" This teaches risk appetite definition.
