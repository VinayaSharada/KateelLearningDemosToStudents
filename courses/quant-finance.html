How to teach with this course

About Demo first, actual demo second

Every demo card opens the About Demo page. From there, students can launch the interactive app when they are ready to practice.

1 Read

Understand the objective, concepts, and expected output.

2 Practice

Launch the actual demo and change inputs or scenarios.

3 Reflect

Explain the insight, limitation, and next action.

Learning outcomes

What this course builds

Core outcomes

  • Connect quantitative formulas to business decisions.
  • Interpret option, portfolio, and fixed-income sensitivities.
  • Use simulation to reason about uncertainty and convergence.
  • Explain risk-return trade-offs in plain language.

Faculty prompt

Use a before/after discussion: what does the number mean for an investment, hedge, or funding decision?
