Learning outcomes

What this course pack builds

Core outcomes

  • Distinguish descriptive, predictive, and generative AI workflows.
  • Compare human-only, analytics-first, and AI-assisted decisions.
  • Evaluate usefulness, explainability, and governance signals in AI outputs.
  • Design a workflow with clear human checkpoints rather than blind automation.

AB Testing Framework

All Levels

Your team claims a new feature increases engagement. But did it, or did randomness fool you? Design AB tests with proper controls, sample sizes, and significance thresholds. Learn why most "winning" features are false positives without rigorous experimental design.

Teacher cue: Ask: "How many users do you need to see a real effect? What's the cost of a false positive vs. a false negative?" This reveals statistical rigor and business trade-offs.

AI Cost Benefit Analyzer

Intermediate

Your CFO asks: "Why invest $2M in AI when we don't know the payoff?" Build a business case by modeling infrastructure costs, labor savings, revenue uplift, and risk. See how hidden assumptions (adoption rates, productivity gains) drive ROI—and how small changes swing the decision.

Teacher cue: Challenge the assumptions: "What if adoption is 40% instead of 80%? What if labor savings never materialize?" This teaches sensitivity analysis and the power of assumptions in business decisions.
