Teaching flow

Start from the pack, not the folder tree

This course pack collects the curated public demos for this theme and links them to the right supporting course and assignment resources.

1 Open a demo unit

Use the About page for context, expectations, and teaching prompts.

2 Launch when ready

Move into the interactive demo only after the setup and run mode make sense for the class.

3 Extend with assignments

Use the linked assignment scaffold when you want written analysis, reflection, or grading.

Learning outcomes

What this course pack builds

Core outcomes

  • Distinguish descriptive, predictive, and generative AI workflows.
  • Compare human-only, analytics-first, and AI-assisted decisions.
  • Evaluate usefulness, explainability, and governance signals in AI outputs.
  • Design a workflow with clear human checkpoints rather than blind automation.

Audience fit

AI product, governance, workflow design

Curated public packClassroom ready

Faculty prompt

Have students toggle AI on and off, then explain not just what changed in the answer, but what changed in the workflow and oversight burden.
