How to teach with this course

About Demo first, actual demo second

Every demo card opens the About Demo page. From there, students can launch the interactive app when they are ready to practice.

1 Read

Understand the objective, concepts, and expected output.

2 Practice

Launch the actual demo and change inputs or scenarios.

3 Reflect

Explain the insight, limitation, and next action.

Learning outcomes

What this course builds

Core outcomes

  • Distinguish descriptive, predictive, and generative AI use cases.
  • Compare human, statistical, and AI-assisted decisions.
  • Evaluate model outputs for usefulness, bias, and explainability.
  • Design an AI workflow with clear human oversight.

Faculty prompt

Have students toggle AI on/off and document what changed in the recommendation, not just the final answer.
