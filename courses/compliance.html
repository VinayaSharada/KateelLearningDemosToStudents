How to teach with this course

About Demo first, actual demo second

Every demo card opens the About Demo page. From there, students can launch the interactive app when they are ready to practice.

1 Read

Understand the objective, concepts, and expected output.

2 Practice

Launch the actual demo and change inputs or scenarios.

3 Reflect

Explain the insight, limitation, and next action.

Learning outcomes

What this course builds

Core outcomes

  • Translate regulatory expectations into operational controls.
  • Prioritize alerts using risk, evidence, and escalation logic.
  • Assess AI governance using scorecards and accountability roles.
  • Connect compliance evidence to audit-ready documentation.

Faculty prompt

Ask students to produce a one-page control note: risk, evidence, owner, escalation, and residual risk.
