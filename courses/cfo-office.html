Why this overlay exists

Use one demo library, but frame it for executive finance decisions

This overlay does not replace Treasury, AI/ML, Compliance, or Payments packs. It re-curates selected demos from those packs so finance leaders can work from the decision they face rather than from the folder that owns the demo.

1 Start with the decision

Pick the demo based on value, risk, controls, close, evidence, or working-capital questions.

2 Use the About page first

Frame the business decision, limitations, owner, and human approval point before launch.

3 Reuse across stakeholders

The same demo can serve CFOs, treasury, controllership, transformation teams, and faculty when the framing is explicit.

What this builds

Core CFO overlay outcomes

Decision quality

  • Evaluate value, risk, control, stakeholder impact, and next decision together.
  • Separate finance evidence from unsupported claims and black-box confidence.
  • Decide what can be standardized or automated and what must remain human-reviewed.

Audience fit

CFOs, aspiring CFOs, controllership, treasury, finance transformation, internal audit, faculty, and executive-education participants.

Overlay themes

Value case, governance, evidence, workflow, close, reconciliation, O2C/P2P, collections, and treasury operating discipline.
