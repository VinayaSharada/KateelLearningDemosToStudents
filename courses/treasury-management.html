Learning outcomes

What this course pack builds

Core outcomes

  • Explain how treasury analytics supports short-horizon cash and hedge decisions.
  • Compare FX, stablecoin, smart-contract, and working-capital trade-offs across scenarios.
  • Use dashboard, simulation, and scenario outputs to defend a treasury action.
  • Separate raw metrics from managerial judgment when AI suggestions are present.

AI Hedge Orchestrator

Intermediate

Your multinational has €50M in revenue exposure to EUR/USD volatility. AI recommends a hedge—but at what ratio and cost? Decide between full/partial hedges and learn why underhedging can destroy shareholder value faster than overhedging.

Teacher cue: Emphasize the tradeoff: cheaper partial hedges leave you exposed. Why would a CFO accept residual risk? When do you fully hedge vs. layer hedges over time?

Treasury Analytics Notebook Hub

Intermediate to Advanced

Move from spreadsheets to analytics. Walk through a multi-week treasury forecasting workflow: data prep → anomaly detection → cash flow forecasting → dashboard insights. Learn how to catch forecast drift before it hits operations and how to automate weekly reporting that treasury teams can act on.

Teacher cue: Push on this: "Your forecast was right 20 times but wrong 2 times catastrophically. Why trust it?" This reveals the gap between statistical accuracy and operational reliability—data quality matters more than model complexity.

Mesa Liquidity Data Generator

Beginner to Intermediate

Build a bank's balance sheet: customer deposits, savings rates, withdrawal patterns. Then stress it: what if 10%, 20%, 30% of customers withdraw simultaneously? Monte Carlo shows the range of liquidity outcomes. Learn how asset-liability managers size funding buffers and when to tap contingency facilities.

Teacher cue: Emphasize the behavioral assumption: "You're assuming customers behave historically. But what if crisis sentiment changes behavior?" This connects data science to operational reality—models assume conditions stay stable when stress breaks that assumption.

CCC Analyzer

Beginner to Intermediate

You collect from customers in 45 days but must pay suppliers in 30 days. That 15-day gap ties up working capital. See how DSO, DIO, and DPO interact—and why a 10-day improvement in receivables can unlock millions in cash without borrowing.

Teacher cue: Push on this: "If you improve collections by 10 days, how much cash is freed up?" Then show the compounding effect across the quarter. Make it real with balance sheet impact.

Dell vs Competitors Working Capital Case

Beginner to Intermediate

Why does Dell unlock billions in cash faster than HP? Compare receivables, inventory, and payables cycles across the PC industry. See how Dell's direct-to-consumer model compressed CCC from 60 days to negative days—generating free cash while competitors waited. Understand business model impact on treasury.

Teacher cue: Ask: "Can Apple or HP replicate Dell's CCC?" The answer is no—because CCC isn't just a metric, it's a reflection of business model, customer mix, and operational excellence. Treasury is strategy.

FX Hedge Simulator

Beginner to Intermediate

Teacher cue: Show both scenarios: "What if you'd fully hedged at a 2% premium? What if you'd hedged nothing?" This drives home that hedging trades downside insurance for opportunity cost.

Smart Contract Treasury

Beginner to Intermediate

Code becomes law on-chain. Define treasury rules in a smart contract—e.g., "approve wire transfers only if balance > $5M AND approval is dual-signature." No CFO override, no exceptions. Is this efficient or a risk? Learn when to automate treasury vs. when human judgment matters.

Teacher cue: Prompt: "If a smart contract rules fail, who's liable? Can you override it?" This reveals the tension between decentralized governance and operational reality. Rules are immutable—but should treasury ever be?

Stablecoin Manager

Advanced

You issue a stablecoin pegged at $1. Suddenly, $100M in redemptions arrive—everyone wants their USD back. You have enough reserves, but peg pressure forces a choice: raise interest rates to attract new deposits, or hold steady and watch the peg crack? Learn collateral management and reserve mechanics of decentralized stablecoins.

Teacher cue: This is modern monetary policy in real-time. Ask: "How is this different from managing a bank run?" Stablecoins are a treasury function for issuers—liability management under duress.

Treasury Control Tower

Beginner to Intermediate

It's Friday 5 PM. Your cash position is $2M but you're expecting a $10M outflow Monday. The AI forecasts a gap. Do you draw a credit line today (cost: facility fee), defer payments, or trust the forecast to adjust? See how AI-powered liquidity monitoring drives real-time treasury decisions.

Teacher cue: Challenge the forecast: "How confident is this AI model? What happens if it's wrong?" This teaches false confidence—AI is a tool for speed, not certainty. CFOs still own the decision.

Treasury Transform Blueprint

Beginner to Intermediate

Your CFO says "we need to modernize treasury." Where do you start? Dashboard? Automation? API integration? Design a multi-year roadmap that prioritizes quick wins (free up cash now) while building toward future state (real-time insights, autonomous execution). Learn how transformation value compounds across initiatives.

Teacher cue: Challenge sequencing: "Why would you build a dashboard before fixing data quality?" This reveals how treasury transformation requires sequencing—you can't automate garbage data.
