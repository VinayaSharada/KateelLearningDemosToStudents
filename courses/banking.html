How to teach with this course

About Demo first, actual demo second

Every demo card opens the About Demo page. From there, students can launch the interactive app when they are ready to practice.

1 Read

Understand the objective, concepts, and expected output.

2 Practice

Launch the actual demo and change inputs or scenarios.

3 Reflect

Explain the insight, limitation, and next action.

Learning outcomes

What this course builds

Core outcomes

  • Interpret banking analytics in customer, credit, and fraud contexts.
  • Explain model-based decisions with business constraints.
  • Evaluate segmentation, prediction, and risk outputs responsibly.
  • Connect analytics results to operational actions.

Faculty prompt

Use the demo result as the starting point for a customer, risk, or branch-manager decision discussion.
