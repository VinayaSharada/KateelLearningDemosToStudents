Loan Default Predictor

Beginner to Intermediate

You approve 90% of loan applications, but 8% default. Tighten lending and reduce defaults to 5%, but approval rate drops to 70%. Which trade-off is better for profitability? Learn how default prediction shapes portfolio composition, capital allocation, and bank risk-taking appetite.

Teacher cue: Challenge the threshold: "Why not deny everyone? You'd have zero defaults." This reveals the core lending paradox: higher accuracy ≠ higher profits. Portfolio composition matters.

Interest Rate Risk

Intermediate

The Fed raises rates 100 bps. Your deposit costs jump, but loan rates locked in 6 months ago don't move. Asset-liability gap widens and net interest margin compresses. See how deposit mix, repricing speed, and customer behavior shape your funding cost under stress—and why banks hedge with derivatives.

Teacher cue: Ask: "What's the biggest risk: losing deposits or deposit costs spiking?" This connects micro-level customer decisions (whether to leave for higher-yield accounts) to macro-level bank risk management.

Liquidity Management

Beginner to Intermediate

In a crisis, when will you run out of cash? Stress test deposits (what if 20% flee?), forecast outflows (payments, withdrawals), and calculate buffers you need. Monte Carlo shows the range of outcomes—and when to trigger contingency funding. Learn how risk officers set liquidity thresholds that prevent crises before they happen.

Teacher cue: Challenge assumptions: "What if 30% of deposits leave, not 20%? What if the Fed cuts rates faster than you expect?" This teaches how stress tests shape real-time treasury decisions.

Credit Scoring

Beginner to Intermediate

A customer's credit score drops 40 points. Should they be declined? More accurate models approve lower-risk borrowers but deny some good customers. Explore score drivers, pricing trade-offs, and Fair Lending constraints. Why transparent models win in banking—not just for compliance, but for customer trust and revenue.

Teacher cue: Push on explainability: "Why does income matter more than payment history in this model? Can you defend that to regulators?" This teaches model governance and Fair Lending awareness.

Fraud Playground

Intermediate

Block a fraudulent transaction and you save $500. Block a legitimate one and you lose a customer. Explore transaction patterns, anomaly scoring, and threshold trade-offs—the real dilemma fraud teams face every day. Learn why perfect accuracy is impossible (and expensive).

Teacher cue: Ask: "Why would you ever let a transaction through if the model says 'fraud'?" This reveals the operational cost of false positives and drives home the real trade-off: fraud prevention vs. customer experience.

Blockchain Explorer

All Levels

Why are central banks building CBDCs? How do stablecoins disrupt payments? Explore Bitcoin's settlement finality, Ethereum's smart contracts, and DeFi economics. Understand the tech your bank might build on—or compete against. Learn why decentralized finance works differently (and sometimes fails).

Teacher cue: Connect to bank strategy: "If DeFi protocols do settlement in 12 seconds and your bank takes 3 days, how do you respond?" This reveals why banks care about blockchain now, not just someday.
